The Loop · fresh panel ranking · all 39 games

All 39 Deriv games, ranked best → worst

A fresh review pass over the entire collection — original 30 plus the 9 built by the Fable 5 model (marked with a violet Fable chip). Four independent reviewer agents re-rendered every game and scored it 0–10 on four axes: first-frame Visual wow, addictive Loop pull, Originality of the core bet, and how intrinsically the live Market settles it (panel score out of 40 shown per row). Final order blends the panel scores with editorial tie-breaking. Keepers rank first; duplicates and below-bar designs sit under the divider, ranked among themselves. Previews are the live prototypes — click "open ↗" for the full-bleed game.

1
RIPTIDE Keeper panel 33/40 open ↗

Stunning multicolor ink-trail field — price-as-paintbrush territory war is fresh, instantly readable, and entirely settled by the real tick path. The panel's clear #1.
