# CRITERIA.md — what "good" means (FIXED — never edited by the loop)

This is the fixed acceptance bar. The **judge** scores every design against it. The
self-improvement step may **not** edit this file — that is what keeps the bar from
drifting. Tuning happens only in `ITERATION.md` §Tuning.

A design is a **KEEPER** only if it clears **all** gates at a high bar:

- **Visual** — genuinely stunning: polished, alive with motion, striking in its very
  first viewport. Not a wireframe, not flat.
- **Engagement** — interactive, entertaining, addictive. A real Deriv client wants "one
  more round". There is a clear hook and a dopamine loop.
- **Originality (vs. the world)** — no near-identical existing game found in the worker's
  web search. Reskins of saturated genres — Crash, Plinko, Mines, Dice, Limbo,
  Wheel/Roulette, HiLo, Towers, candlestick-clickers, up/down guessing — are **not**
  original.
- **Uniqueness (vs. our keepers)** — a different *core mechanic* from every design already
  kept. Three keepers must be three genuinely different games, not variations on one.
- **Deriv fit** — clearly a trading/fintech game: driven by a live market chart and a real
  bet/payout mechanic.

The experiment is **done when there are 3 keepers**.
