HOW RECOIL WORKS

01 · ANCHOR

Tether the gap, not the price

Two coupled market runners stream live. Their spread breathes on the oscilloscope. Drag the gold anchor to the gap value you believe the pair oscillates around — or let it follow the live gap — and pick a stiffness: stiff tethers store far more energy per stretch but snap on small divergence.

02 · HARVEST

Energy releases on every recoil

While the round runs, the gap stretching away from your anchor charges the tether (energy ∝ stretch², scaled by stiffness). Every time the spread whips back through your anchor, the charge releases into your collector. A becalmed, drifting spread earns nothing — you are paid by oscillation, not by time.

03 · BELL OR SNAP

Survive the divergence

At the 30s bell you bank stake × harvested multiplier. But if the pair decouples and the gap ever stretches past your snap limit, the tether tears — round over, and only 25% of the harvest is salvaged. Tight power or wide safety: choose before you deploy.

RECOIL is a relative-value game: you never bet on a single price going up or down — you bet on the relationship between two instruments. Anchor placement is a read on the pair's equilibrium; stiffness is your risk appetite on correlation holding. Every payout coefficient is tuned to a 95% expected return at all stiffness levels.
