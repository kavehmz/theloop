# MURMUR — herd the market's murmuration home

**One-line pitch:** Sixty starlings ride a jetstream that *is* the live price line — you have eight
pressure pulses and one whistle to shepherd the storm-tossed flock into the roost gate before the
bell; you're paid by headcount delivered.

---

## Skin-free core sentence

> The player's bet is **when and where to spend a limited budget of steering impulses on a swarm of
> autonomous agents that are continuously pushed, lifted and scattered by live price velocity and
> volatility**, and it's settled by **the count of agents standing inside a fixed destination zone
> at the bell, paid through count-based tiers**.

## Core loop (second-to-second)

1. **Stake & release.** Pick a stake, hit **HERD & BET**. Sixty starlings burst from the left.
   The roost gate locks onto the round's opening price — a glowing perch zone at the right edge.
2. **Read the wind.** The live price line *is* a jetstream: birds inside the stream band get
   carried hard toward the gate (tailwind = ride the market), birds outside fight a headwind and
   drift backwards. Price **velocity** lifts or sinks the whole flock (rallies pull birds up,
   dumps drag them down). **Volatility** is turbulence — vol spikes literally blast strays out of
   the stream.
3. **Spend pressure.** You get **8 pressure pulses** (click: birds flee the point, sheepdog-style)
   and **1 whistle** (whole flock surges toward the gate for 1.5 s). Every pulse is a real
   decision: push strays back into the jetstream, or pre-position the flock above the stream
   because momentum is rolling over?
4. **Perch & count.** Birds that reach the gate perch on the wires — banked heads, safe. The
   counter ticks up; the tier ladder lights up live.
5. **The bell.** At 0:00 the round settles on headcount tiers (below). Instant payout flash,
   4-second intermission, next round.

## How the live market drives it

- **Price level → jetstream position.** The carry band is centered on the current price's y. A
  trending market *walks the stream away from your flock* — chasing it costs pulses.
- **Price velocity → vertical wind.** Signed momentum (EMA of returns) is a body force on every
  bird. A sharp dump literally sinks the murmuration below the gate band.
- **Volatility → turbulence.** Realized-vol EMA scales per-bird jitter and gust kicks inside the
  stream: calm tape = silk-smooth ride, violent tape = the stream becomes a blender that pays
  speed but sprays strays.
- **Round-open price → gate band.** The roost is pinned to the open, so the round is implicitly a
  bet that you can keep the flock near the open against whatever trend/vol regime unfolds.

## Bet & payout (60 birds, tiered headcount)

| Heads home | < 15 | 15–24 | 25–34 | 35–43 | 44–51 | 52–60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Payout | 0× | 0.5× | 1.5× | 2.3× | 3.8× | 7× |

Skill moves the distribution: a sloppy herder loses strays to turbulence; a sharp one banks 50+ in
calm tape and still salvages a 1.5× in a storm. Vol regime is visible (turbulence meter), so stake
sizing per regime is itself a skill.

## Why it's addictive

- **Banked progress you can see** — every perched bird is a tick of dopamine; the counter and tier
  ladder light up mid-round, so a round is 60 micro-wins, not one binary outcome.
- **Near-miss agony** — 3 birds short of the next tier with 5 seconds left and one pulse in hand is
  pure "one more round".
- **A live opponent** — the market visibly fights you: you *watch* a red candle rip ten birds out
  of the stream and you *feel* the rescue pulse that saves six of them.
- **Mastery curve** — newcomers spam pulses; regulars learn to herd *ahead* of momentum, save the
  whistle for the final funnel, and read vol regimes before staking.

## Visual / sensory intent

Dusk-indigo sky over a glowing market: ghost candlesticks and price gridlines in the depth layer; a
luminous teal jetstream band flowing along the live price line; 60 starlings with motion trails
murmurating in real time; a warm amber roost gate with perch wires filling up bird by bird; pressure
pulses as expanding shockwave rings; tier ladder and turbulence meter as glass HUD panels. The first
frame is already a storm of motion: stream flowing, flock swirling, candles printing.

## Why it fits Deriv

It is a stake-and-settle round on a live chart where *every* market feature has a mechanical job:
level (stream position), momentum (lift), volatility (turbulence), open (gate). It rewards exactly
the instincts Deriv traders prize — regime reading, momentum anticipation, risk sizing — in a
30-second loop with instant settlement.

## Originality paragraph

Flocking/boid toys exist, and sheepdog games exist, but no found game makes a *market feed* the
physics field that scatters a flock and then sells you a stake on delivered headcount. The decision
(rationed spatial impulses on living agents under price forces) and the settlement (count-based
delivery tiers at a fixed bell) don't reduce to any saturated casino loop: there's no crash scalar,
no grid of mines, no up/down call, no streak combo, no territory share. See `originality.md` for the
adversarial search log.
