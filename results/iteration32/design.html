MURMUR

Sixty starlings ride a jetstream that is the live price line. Momentum lifts and sinks the whole flock, volatility is literal turbulence, and the roost gate is pinned to the round's opening price. You get eight pressure pulses and one whistle — spend them well and get paid by headcount at the bell.

The market is the wind

The glowing stream along the price line carries birds toward the roost — but only while they're inside it. A trending market walks the stream away from your flock; a vol spike blasts strays clean out of it. Outside the stream a headwind pushes birds backwards. Every market feature has a mechanical job: level = stream position, momentum = vertical lift, volatility = turbulence, open = gate height.

Eight pulses, one whistle

Click anywhere: a pressure pulse makes nearby birds flee the point — classic sheepdog herding. Push strays back into the stream, pre-position the flock before a momentum flip, or shove the final stragglers across the headwind into the gate. The whistle surges the whole murmuration toward the roost for 1.5 seconds — most herders save it for the final funnel.

Paid by headcount

Birds that reach the gate perch on the wires — banked, safe, counted live. At the bell your stake settles on delivery tiers, up to 7× for a near-perfect 52+. Three birds short of a tier with one pulse left is the whole game.
