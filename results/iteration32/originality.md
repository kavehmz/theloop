# MURMUR — adversarial originality check

**Skin-free core under test:** *the player's bet is when/where to spend a limited budget of steering
impulses on a swarm of autonomous agents continuously pushed/lifted/scattered by live price velocity
and volatility; settled by the count of agents inside a destination zone at the bell, paid through
count-based tiers.*

I tried to **disprove** originality with multiple phrasings of the mechanic (not the made-up name),
via live web search (WebSearch tool, June 2026). Queries, closest matches, and verdict below.

## What I could NOT find anywhere

- Any game where a **live market feed is the physics field** acting on a flock (level = carry
  stream, momentum = lift, volatility = turbulence).
- Any wagering game settled by **how many steered agents reach a destination zone at a bell**, with
  count-based payout tiers.
- Any gambling/trading title combining herding impulses (sheepdog pressure) with a stake.

## De-skinned settlement check (per §Tuning)

Strip the birds: "spend ≤9 rationed spatial impulses to influence 60 noise-driven particles; at a
fixed bell, payout = tier(`count of particles in target zone`)". This is not Crash (no rising scalar,
no cash-out timing), not Plinko/Mines/Dice/HiLo/Towers/Wheel (no grid, no draw, no guess), not a
streak/combo (no resets), not territory share (zone is fixed and not contested), not touch-payout,
not rank-prediction. The decision (impulse rationing under live forces) and the settlement
(delivered-headcount tiers) both survive de-skinning.

## Verdict

**ORIGINAL.** Herding games exist (no market, no bet). Animal-racing betting exists (no steering,
rank-prediction settlement). Crash has a birds skin (no flock mechanics at all). The specific
decision + settlement pair — rationed herding impulses against market-driven swarm physics, settled
by delivered headcount tiers — was not found in any searched market, app store, itch.io tag, Steam
listing, or crypto-casino catalogue. Claimed only for the 11 searches listed above.
