# RIPTIDE — Adversarial originality check

**Core mechanic being tested (not the made-up name):** a real-time game where the play field is a *live price chart*, the vertical price axis is divided into claimable territory, and the **price tick path itself paints/captures area** in the color of whichever claim it currently passes through; you bet on holding the **largest share of painted area** (spatial coverage over time) when a round locks, with mid-round abilities (surge/pull) to expand or move your claim. It is NOT up/down, NOT a multiplier/crash, NOT buying priced grid cells, NOT a single reveal.

I tried to **disprove** originality by searching for each of the three constituent ideas and their fusion: (1) ink/area territory capture, (2) on a live market price chart, (3) as a real-money bet on coverage.

## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**

I could not find any existing game — web, images, app stores, itch.io, ProductHunt, Reddit/YouTube (via search), or X — that combines **ink-coverage territory capture** with **a live market price line as the capturing agent** and **a real-money bet on spatial dominance of the chart over time**. The territory-painting genre (Paper.io, Territorial.io, INTVL) never touches markets or price; the market/chart-betting genre (Crash, Polymarket, trading sims) never expresses the bet as *who controls the most painted area of the price field*. RIPTIDE's specific fusion — and especially the idea that the price tick path is the brush that decides ownership of contested horizontal price-bands, scored as a graded coverage % at lock — appears genuinely novel.

**Honesty caveats:** I relied on text search results and two X queries; a tiny, poorly-indexed indie/Discord prototype could exist that I did not surface. The *conceptual primitives* (territory painting; chart betting) are each well known — the originality claim is specifically about their **combination on a price field with price-as-painter**, which I did not find anywhere.
