How it plays

The price line is the brush. Hold the most painted ocean when the tide locks.
01 · CLAIM

Drop your band

Drag your gold price-band to where you think the market will live. Tighter band = denser reward but easier to miss; wider = safer, lower multiplier.

02 · PAINT

The market inks

Every tick, the glowing price comet lays ink in the color of whichever band it's inside. No dice — only real price movement captures territory.

03 · CONTEST

Surge & pull

Spend a slice of stake to balloon your band for 3s or drag it toward momentum. Rival tides do the same — overlaps shimmer and split.

04 · LOCK

Tally the tide

The field freezes and every inked pixel is counted. Payout = pool × your coverage share × volatility multiplier. Dominate a wild tide, win big.
