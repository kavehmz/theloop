How DEEPLINE works

01 · CAST

Choose your depth

Depth = price level. The live price is a fish swimming up & down the column. Drag the hook to a band and cast. Deeper bands pay more but the fish fights harder.

02 · HOOK

The strike

When the price touches your hook depth the fish strikes. The market's volatility at that instant sets the fish size — choppy moments hook the trophies.

03 · FIGHT

Reel vs. tension

Reel toward the boat to gain distance — but reeling against a price run loads the line toward SNAP. Feed slack through the runs, crank in the calm. Read the tape.

04 · LAND

Bank or break

Reel to the surface to land the full payout, or NET EARLY for the banked distance. Let tension hit the redline and the line snaps — the fish bolts with your stake.
