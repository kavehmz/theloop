*core mechanic* (cast a hook to a depth that maps to live price level → strike on a price-level

crypto-casino aggregators, and trading/Deriv sources. Verdict at the bottom. I claim only what I

## Honest assessment of the single closest things

1. **Tension/slack reel-fight (Project Zomboid / Fishing Planet / Sea of Thieves):** the fight *feel* is
   genuine prior art — but those are PvE fishing sims with scripted fish and no money, no chart, no
   price. DEEPLINE makes the fish's runs literally the live price velocity and the catch a real bet.
2. **Big Bass Crash:** the closest *gambling* product wearing a fishing skin — but it IS Crash
   (rising multiplier, cash out before bust). DEEPLINE deliberately avoids that frame.
3. **Fish-table shooters / fishing slots:** different mechanics entirely (shooting / RNG reels).

No product found maps **depth → live price level**, makes the **strike a real price-level touch**,
scales the **catch by realised volatility**, and resolves it through a **two-sided tension fight
against real price velocity** as a **real-money trading bet on a live market chart**. The combination
is unoccupied.

## VERDICT: **ORIGINAL**

Why: The individual ingredients exist in separate worlds — a tension-reel fight lives in fishing
*sims*; rising-multiplier cash-out lives in *Crash* clones (incl. a fishing-skinned one); price-level
*touch* bets live in *options trading*. DEEPLINE is the first I can find to fuse them: a live market
chart rendered as a water column where price = depth, the price tick is the quarry, a level-touch is
the hook/strike, realised volatility is the fish size, and the payout is *fight distance* sculpted in
real time by the trade-off between reeling and feeding slack against actual price velocity — with the
loss being an actively-managed line snap, not a scalar climbing until a random bust. It is not a
reskin of any banned genre (no Crash/Plinko/Mines/Dice/Limbo/Wheel/HiLo/Towers/up-down), and its core
is distinct from the fishing-skinned Crash competitor. Residual risk: the reel-fight *feel* will
remind fishing-sim players of Fishing Planet, so messaging should foreground the live-market driver.
