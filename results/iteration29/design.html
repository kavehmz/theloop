EMBERWELL — pick where the market will smoulder

Drop a glowing band on the price ladder. Wherever the live price lingers inside your well, it stokes an ember and HEAT pours in at a rate set by how narrow you made the band. When price wanders off, the ember cools and HEAT bleeds away — but never to zero, so you keep what you banked. At the 30-second bell, your payout is the heat you accumulated. The bet is a single, deeply second-guessable read: where will price want to rest, and how tightly dare you call it?

NOT STAY-INSIDE

One exit doesn't kill you. Dwell accrues continuously; leaving just cools the ember. Almost every round banks something.

WIDTH IS YOUR RISK DIAL

A razor band on a consolidation shelf burns toward a 9× ember. A fat safe band is gentle but lands far more often.

CHASE OR HOLD

Twice a round you can re-cast the well to follow the drift — for a HEAT tax. The live read-and-chase dilemma is the hook.
