# ASSAY — Originality check (adversarial)

## What I was trying to disprove
That ASSAY's **core mechanic** already exists somewhere as a game:
> set RATIOS across multiple **live price-driven** ingredients (valve vector) to match a **market-drifting TARGET compound**, settled by **distance-to-target in a continuous blend space** as a real-money bet.

I searched for the mechanic in many phrasings (mixing/blending/alloy/potion/color/ternary/allocation), plus the trading-casino and Deriv space, not just the name "ASSAY."

## Closest matches found (with links) and why each differs

**A. Color / RGB slider matchers (puzzle, casual)**
- Paint Match — https://ombosoft.itch.io/paint-match
- RGB Match — https://onlyrevolutions.itch.io/rgb-match
- Color Loop — https://el15.itch.io/color-loop
- Trycolors "Guess the Mix" — https://trycolors.com/games/guess-mix
- Mix to Match — https://mixtomatch.org/
  *Differs:* you mix to a **static** target with sliders that ARE the channels. No live signal drives ingredient potency, the target never drifts with an external feed, there is no stake/payout, and time pressure (if any) is arbitrary, not market-driven. ASSAY's sliders are *ratios over live signals whose strength changes under you*, and the goal *moves with the market*.

**B. Crafting "blend-to-target" games (alloy / potion)**
- Vintage Story alloy crucible (mix metals to a % target, turns "VALID" green) — https://mods.vintagestory.at/realloy ; calculator: https://cal2.calculator.city/alloy-calculator-vintage-story/
- Potion Craft: Alchemist Simulator (move a marker on an alchemy map toward a target zone) — https://store.steampowered.com/app/1210320/Potion_Craft_Alchemist_Simulator/
  *Differs:* These are the strongest *conceptual* cousins (a spatial target you steer a blend toward). But they are **single-player crafting puzzles**: the target is a **fixed recipe**, ingredients have **fixed** values, there is **no live market**, **no continuous drift**, and **no real bet/payout**. ASSAY converts the same "blend toward a point" intuition into a *market-reactive, money-settled* round where both your ingredient potencies and the target are live functions of price texture.

**C. Color-prediction betting apps (real money)**
- Jalwa / color prediction 2026 — https://finance.yahoo.com/news/color-prediction-game-2026-color-094300914.html
- Color Bet (Mohio) — https://mohiogaming.com/games/lotteries/color-bet
- Super Color Game (Evolution) — https://www.evolution.com/games/super-color-game/
  *Differs:* These are **pick-one-color RNG bets** (a HiLo/roulette cousin). No mixing, no ratios, no continuous blend space, no live market feed. The opposite of a proportion game.

**D. Crypto/trading-casino & Deriv**
- Shuffle Limbo / Crash / Dice / Plinko — https://shuffle.com/games/originals/limbo
- Deriv synthetic & hybrid indices (instruments "blend" vol/trend, but that's the *feed design*, not a player game) — https://deriv.com/academy/trading-guides/deriv-launches-hybrid-indices-for-synthetic-trading
  *Differs:* The trading-casino canon is the banned saturated set (Crash/Limbo/Dice/Plinko/etc.). None is a ratio-mixing-to-target game. Deriv's own "blending" refers to how an index is generated, not to a mixing decision the player makes.

## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**
The *theme* (alloy/potion/color mixing toward a target) is well-trodden in crafting and casual puzzles, and color-prediction betting exists — but the **decision+settlement core** of ASSAY is not found anywhere:
**a vector of mixing ratios across LIVE market-derived ingredients whose potencies shift in real time, matched against a TARGET compound that DRIFTS with the live feed, settled by distance-to-target in a ternary blend space as a real-money bet.**
That specific combination — proportion/blend-to-target wired intrinsically to live price facets and a payout curve — appears nowhere in my searches. The nearest real game (Potion Craft) shares only the "steer a blend toward a point" intuition and lacks the live feed, drift, and bet entirely. ASSAY also occupies the PROPORTION/MIXING family that none of the kept cores touch (it is not phase-locking a single signal, not routing a flow, not claiming levels/bands).
