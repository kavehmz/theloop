How a round works

01 — READ

Three facets, one feed

The live feed is split into Trend, Volatility and Momentum. Each becomes a molten stream whose potency rises and falls with the market's character — not its direction.

02 — MIX

Set the ratios

Drag the three valves to pour your blend. The crucible's colour is your live alloy. A pulsing target swatch drifts as the market moves — steer your blend point onto it inside the alloy field.

03 — CAST

Lock & settle

At lock, distance between your blend and the target sets purity. Above the line the ingot rings and pays a convex multiplier up to a flawless 12×; below it, the ingot fractures and the stake is lost.

The bet (skin-free): a vector of mixing ratios across multiple live price-driven ingredients, settled by how closely the resulting blend matches a live-drifting target compound at lock. It is proportion-matching in a continuous blend space — not level-claiming, region-drawing, flow-routing, or riding a rising multiplier.
