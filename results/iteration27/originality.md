# Originality check — DETONATE

**Core mechanic being tested (skin-free):** the player *pre-seeds a fixed set of charges across price-space*; the *live price path is the only ignition source*; a touched charge *chain-triggers every other charge within a blast radius* (neighbour-propagation); payout = *the size of the single largest CONNECTED cascade that actually fires*. The bet is on chain TOPOLOGY/connectivity in price-space, order-irrelevant. I searched multiple phrasings of this mechanic — not the made-up name "DETONATE".

## Method
Adversarial web searches (Google web + the surfaced image/aggregator results), targeting trading-casino sites, crypto P2E, slot cascade engines, and general game catalogues, plus reasoning over what the surfaced Reddit/YouTube/app-store/itch/ProductHunt-class results contained. Goal: try to DISPROVE originality.

## Queries run (and what came back)

1. **"trading game place charges price chart chain reaction cascade detonate connected blast bet payout"**
   - *Stop Cascade Simulation* — https://stopcascade.netlify.app/ — a sim of real stop-loss cascades (place stop orders, fire a market order, watch a wick). It is a market-microstructure educational toy, **not a bet**, no player-seeded blast-radius graph, no connected-cascade payout. Closest *conceptual* cousin (chain of triggers in a price move) but not a game and not the mechanic.
   - *CoinPoker Crypto Futures* — https://coinpoker.com/crypto-futures/ — pick-a-multiplier price bet. Up/down/multiplier wager; no seeding, no cascade.
   - *Reactoonz / nuclear-reactor slots* (via btcgosu guide) — cascading-symbol slots: RNG symbols tumble on a fixed grid, chain wins charge a meter. **No price input, no player-authored placement, no spatial graph** — the only "cascade" overlap is a slot tumble engine, structurally unrelated.

2. **"crypto trading game seed mines bombs chain explosion combo price level payout connected cluster"**
   - *Duel Mines* — https://duelcasinoreview.com/mines/ — Mines: reveal independent tiles, hit a bomb = lose. No propagation between tiles, no player-chosen geometry in price-space, not price-driven. (Mines is also a banned genre — and DETONATE is not it: charges are placed by the player and fire by neighbour-chaining off the live price path.)
   - *Bomb Crypto (BCOIN)* — https://bombcrypto.io/ , https://chainplay.gg/games/bomb-crypto/ — an NFT P2E grid game where heroes plant bombs to break blocks for tokens. Session-based RPG/idle, no live market chart, no bet on cascade size. Name-collision only.
   - *Shuffle Mines* — https://shuffle.com/games/originals/mines — same Mines mechanic as above.

3. **"place explosives on price chart price triggers chain reaction biggest combo wins betting game forex synthetic"**
   - Returns only trading-education content: chart-pattern courses, "combo trigger" price-action strategies (Forex School Online, PriceAction.com, IG, FXNX). These describe *real-trading* combo setups, not a game with seeded charges and cascade payout. No match.

4. **'"chain reaction" OR cascade betting game live market price path detonates planted nodes spatial clustering payout'**
   - *Stop Cascade Simulation* again (same as #1).
   - *Chain Reaction (game show)* — https://gameshows.fandom.com/wiki/Chain_Reaction — word-association TV show, unrelated.
   - Academic papers on order-flow branching/cascades in markets (arXiv) — describe the *real* phenomenon DETONATE gamifies, confirming the metaphor is grounded, but none is a game.
   - The model explicitly noted no result combined seeded nodes + spatial clustering + live price path + cascade-size payout.

## Closest existing things, ranked
1. **Slot "cascade/tumble" engines (Reactoonz, cluster-pays slots)** — share the word "cascade" and chain-win visuals, but cascades are RNG symbols on a fixed grid; the player authors nothing and no price drives it. Structurally different settlement (line/cluster pays per tumble vs largest connected fired component the player placed). Not a match.
2. **Mines (Duel/Shuffle)** — shares "hidden things on a board you can set off," but tiles are independent (no propagation), positions aren't player-chosen in price-space, and it isn't price-driven. Banned genre; DETONATE is categorically not it.
3. **Stop Cascade Simulation** — the only thing that genuinely chains triggers from a price move, but it's a non-betting microstructure sim with no player seeding and no connected-cascade scoring.

## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**
No shipped game found that combines all three load-bearing pillars: (a) the **player seeds a charge graph in price-space** before the round; (b) the **live price path is the sole, RNG-free ignition trigger**; (c) **neighbour-propagation cascades** settle the bet by the **size of the largest connected fired component** (order-irrelevant graph connectivity). Cascade slots cover (c)-flavoured visuals without (a) or (b); Mines covers a board-of-hazards without propagation, player-placed price geometry, or price drive; stop-cascade sims cover real chained triggers without being a seedable bet. The combination — a graph-connectivity wager settled by what shape the live price draws through a field you wired — was not found anywhere in the searches above.

*Caveat: I ran web searches (Google + surfaced aggregator results); I did not separately authenticate into X/Reddit/app-store consoles. Within the results actually surfaced across these phrasings, no near-identical game appeared.*
