Why PRISMA

🌈 Volatility you can aim at

Every other trading game bets on a number going up. PRISMA bets on the shape of dispersion: high volatility fans the price-light wide across the spectrum, calm focuses it to a pencil. You are literally claiming where variance will scatter.

⚡ The market is the optics

Beam height = price level. Incoming angle = velocity. Fan spread = realized volatility. Fan tilt = drift. Nothing is decoration — the light path is the tape, refracted through glass.

💎 Skill in the cold bands

Read a vol regime before it breaks and pre-claim the cold extremes — when the spike fans the light out to them, a 25× band detonates. Calm read? Aim the focused pencil dead-on. The FOCUS LENS lets you tighten variance on demand.
