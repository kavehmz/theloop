How RAMPART plays

01 · DEPLOY

Build the fan

A fan of seven bastions is laid at price levels straddling your entry. Spend a finite garrison of armor — thick walls soak more, thin walls fall fast. Tight spreads sit in the line of fire for a high multiplier; wide spreads are rarely touched but pay little.

02 · SIEGE

The market attacks

The price is the army. Every adverse excursion that drives into a wall deals damage = volatility of the move × how far it penetrates. Sharp, fast moves are heavy assaults; calm drifts barely scratch. Walls crack, spall, and collapse — the next wall becomes the front line.

03 · HOLD

Triage & settle

Reinforcement trickles back — tap the wall under fire to patch it, or save it for the front the price is charging. At the horn your payout = stake × (1 + fraction of total wall HP still standing × multiplier). Hold everything for the max; lose it all and you're overrun.

Skin-free core: the player's bet is how to distribute and reinforce a depletable armor budget across many independent fortified price-levels, settled by the fraction of total wall HP still standing when the timed siege ends — every adverse price excursion deals damage scaled by volatility × penetration. Touching a level hurts you; nothing is claimed or banked. It is real-time spatial damage-absorption: tower-defense triage where the live price feed is the literal attacking force.
