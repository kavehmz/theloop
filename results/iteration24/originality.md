- **Not Crash/Aviator:** no scalar climbing to a cash-out before a bust; outcome is the *spatial fortification state* across many walls at the horn.

## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**

The general genre "tower defense with crypto skin" exists (Crypto: Against All Odds and the NFT-TD aggregators), and "walls / holding the line against volatility" is an established *trading metaphor* (GEX call/put walls). But the specific fusion that defines RAMPART — a **real-money bet** where the player allocates and live-reinforces a **depletable armor budget across discrete price-level walls**, the **live price's adverse excursions are the literal attacking damage (volatility × penetration)**, and the bet is **settled by the fraction of total wall HP surviving a timed siege** — was not found in any existing game, app, itch.io entry, ProductHunt/Steam listing, or crypto-trading-game product. The closest real product (Crypto: Against All Odds) uses RNG waves with price as a mild income modifier and has no stake/HP-fraction settlement, making it categorically different. Original.
