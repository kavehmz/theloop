# HOTHOUSE

**One-line pitch:** A living greenhouse built over a live market chart — plant four species of
market-fed flora (bull-vines drink rallies, umbra ferns drink sell-offs, stormcaps feed on
volatility, moonpetals feed on calm), then prune & graft sap between plots as the market's
"weather" shifts; at the bell only plants that crossed a bloom threshold pay, and a plant one
stage short of bloom is worth a fraction of one that made it.

---

## Skin-free core sentence

> The player's bet is **an allocation of stake across slots, each assigned one of four
> accumulator types that accrue progress only from a specific CHARACTER of live price action
> (up-moves / down-moves / high realized volatility / low realized volatility), plus a small
> budget of mid-round transfer actions that move accrued progress from one slot to another at
> a haircut** — and it's settled **at a fixed bell by mapping each slot's final progress
> through nonlinear stage thresholds, where only the upper stages pay and the top stage pays
> disproportionately**.

### Diff against every kept core (1–27)

1. **THREADLINE** (containment corridor) — no corridor, no containment; growth comes from return
   character, not path containment. Different decision, different settlement.
2. **REACTOR** (accumulate vs rupture, bank before blow) — no rupture meter, no bust, no banking
   verb; settlement is at a fixed bell via stage thresholds. Not a cash-out race.
3. **RIPTIDE** (price paints claimable territory) — no spatial claims, no territory share.
4. **SLINGSHOT VEGA** (ballistic launch, distance gates) — no physics launch; thresholds are on an
   accrued regime-integral per slot, not on a projectile's reach, and the decision is allocation +
   transfers, not charge/launch timing.
5. **DEADLOCK** (two-force tension, push/lock) — no tension meter, no redline.
6. **SLIPSTREAM** (pilot through terrain) — no piloting.
7. **PULSEPOINT** (tap reversals, streak combo) — no timing taps, no streak that resets.
8. **TOLLGATE** (own price levels, paid on touch) — slots are not price levels; price touching
   anything is irrelevant — only the *statistical character* of returns feeds growth.
9. **BALLAST** (stack vs topple, bank height) — no stacking, no topple.
10. **APOAPSIS** (hold orbital band, bank laps) — no band-holding.
11. **STARFORGE** (price threads ordered nodes) — no path-through-targets.
12. **COREBORE** (steer drill, extract, escape) — no steering/escape.
13. **SLUICE** (route flow through authored pipes into basins) — superficially "market feature
    drives stuff" but the decision there is authoring a routing network and the payout is which
    basin fluid lands in; here the decision is type-allocation per slot and payout is per-slot
    threshold stages. No routing, no network.
14. **WAKELINE** (pre-trace path, paid by fidelity) — no path prediction.
15. **FLUXYARD** (classify streaming regime parcels, paid by correct-sort throughput) — the closest
    family by *fuel* (regimes), but the loop is opposite: FLUXYARD is reactive real-time
    classification labor paid per correct sort; HOTHOUSE is a pre-round portfolio of regime
    accumulators settled by nonlinear thresholds, with a scarce transfer verb. You never label
    anything; being "right" means your *allocation* matched the regime mix, and mid-round skill is
    moving accrued progress, not sorting items.
16. **OVERTONE** (phase-lock a wave, Q compounds) — no tuning/locking.
17. **STOWLINE** (pack cargo, coverage density) — no packing.
18. **ASSAY** (set mixing ratios to match a drifting target, paid by closeness at lock) — also
    ratio-flavored, but ASSAY pays by *closeness of your mix to a target* at lock; HOTHOUSE has no
    target to match — each slot independently accrues from regime character and pays through its
    own stage thresholds. Settlement function is entirely different (per-slot nonlinear stages vs
    global distance-to-target).
19. **SPREADKEEPER** (quote bid/ask, capture spread) — no quoting.
20. **RAMPART** (armor budget across walls under siege, paid by surviving HP) — budget-allocation
    flavor exists, but RAMPART's slots ARE price levels eroded by price touching them (defensive,
    proximity-driven); HOTHOUSE slots are level-free accumulators fed by return character
    (generative, regime-driven), and it adds the graft-transfer verb and stage-threshold payout
    instead of surviving-HP payout.
21. **DETONATE** (cascade of seeded charges) — no chain reactions in price-space.
22. **SILKLINE** (predict finishing order) — no ranking.
23. **EMBERWELL** (placed band accrues leaky dwell-time heat, banked at bell) — the one to beat,
    and explicitly designed against: EMBERWELL's accrual is *price proximity to a placed band*
    (dwell time in a region of price-space). HOTHOUSE has **no bands and no proximity**: a plant
    grows identically whether price is at 1000 or 1100 — all that matters is whether returns are
    up, down, wild, or quiet. Decision is *which regime-harvesters to field where* (and grafting),
    not *where to put a band*. Settlement is nonlinear stage cliffs per slot, not banked
    continuous heat.
24. **SUMMIT FLAGS** (call the extremum's coordinates) — no point prediction.
25. **CRUX** (irreversible ratchet-locks at live level) — no level locks.
26. **MURMUR** (steer a swarm, headcount tiers) — no steering of price-driven agents.
27. **WINDLEASE** (buy future time windows, paid by realized travel inside them) — no time-interval
    ownership.

Also not poker scoring, not peak-combo, not memory recall, not triage-of-decaying-objects (plants
decay slightly when unfed, but the verb is a scarce 2-click transfer on 6 slots, not frantic
parallel upkeep of many objects, and payout is threshold stages, not kept-alive count), not
band-claiming, not hook-tension.

## Core loop (second to second)

1. **Plant (5–10 s):** Set a stake. Click any of 6 greenhouse plots to cycle its seed:
   🜚 Bullvine (feeds on up-ticks) → Umbra Fern (down-ticks) → Stormcap (high realized vol) →
   Moonpetal (low vol / calm) → empty. Stake splits across planted plots. Hit **PLANT & GROW**.
2. **Grow (40 s):** A synthetic index streams live behind the greenhouse. Its second-to-second
   *character* — measured from actual returns, not labels — feeds the matching species: rallies
   pour growth into bullvines, chop into stormcaps, dead-quiet drift into moonpetals. Unfed plants
   slowly wilt. The "market weather" gauges (trend needle + volatility dial) tell you what the
   air tastes like right now.
3. **Prune & graft (the skill verb):** You carry **3 shears** per round. Click a plant, then click
   another plot: the first is cut to the stump and **65 % of its accrued growth flows into the
   target** (35 % is lost as sap). The read: "the storm is over, my stormcap will never bloom —
   cut it and graft its mass into the moonpetal before the calm stretch ends." Timing and choosing
   the right donor/recipient is everything.
4. **The bell:** Each plant settles by the stage it reached — **Sprout pays 0×, Bud 0.4×,
   Bloom 1.5×, Prime Bloom 3.5×** of that plot's stake. The thresholds are cliffs: a plant at 60 %
   (one tick short of Bloom) pays like a Bud. Harvest bursts, balance updates, replant.

## How the live market drives it

Growth is computed from the tick stream itself: per-tick return `r` and an EMA of `|r|`
(realized vol). Bullvine intensity = `max(r,0)`, Umbra = `max(−r,0)`, Stormcap =
`max(vol − v₀, 0)`, Moonpetal = `max(v₁ − vol, 0)`. The simulated index runs a hidden
regime-switching engine (rally / slide / storm / calm, 4–9 s dwells) so the weather genuinely
turns mid-round — which is exactly what makes grafting a real decision rather than a gimmick.
Nothing depends on price *level*; everything depends on price *behavior*.

## Bet & payout

- Stake split evenly across planted plots (1–6 plots → concentration vs diversification is itself
  a bet on regime persistence).
- Per plot: Sprout (<30) 0×, Bud (≥30) 0.4×, Bloom (≥62) 1.5×, Prime (≥92) 3.5×.
- Tuned so a passively planted, occasionally-matched plant lands around Bud/early-Bloom; reading
  the weather and grafting decisively is what reaches Prime. Skill expresses as EV.

## Why it's addictive

- **Cliff thresholds** create agonizing near-misses ("it was at 89!") and euphoric photo-finish
  blooms — the strongest one-more-round engine there is, and it's earned by play, not RNG alone.
- **The graft dilemma** is a constant micro-trade: cutting a living plant *feels* expensive
  (you watch 35 % evaporate) but holding a wilting one is worse. Every regime flip asks the
  question again.
- **Regime literacy is a learnable skill** — exactly the skill Deriv traders flatter themselves
  on: "I can tell a real trend from chop." The game pays that read directly.

burning through the middle of the frame, six ceramic plots with procedurally-drawn flora

(auto-demo plays until you touch it).

## Deriv fit

It is a stake-and-settle round on a live synthetic index, and the *entire* edge is reading live
market microstructure: trend vs chop vs quiet. It's the regime-recognition instinct of a
multiplier/volatility-index trader, made into a garden you can touch.

## Originality (one paragraph)

Betting *on the statistical character of returns* (direction, vol, calm) rather than on price
level or path is rare even in the trading-game space; wrapping that in a portfolio-of-accumulators
with a scarce, lossy mid-round *transfer* verb and cliff-staged settlement appears genuinely new.
Farming games grow plants with timers and watering; no found game grows them from live market
regime features, and no found betting game settles a regime-fed integral through nonlinear
maturity cliffs with a graft mechanic. See `originality.md` for the adversarial search log.
