# HOTHOUSE — adversarial originality check

Goal: **disprove** originality. The core, skin-free: *allocate stake across slots typed to
four regime-character accumulators (up-trend / down-trend / high vol / low vol), spend a small
budget of lossy mid-round transfers between slots, settle each slot at a fixed bell through
nonlinear stage thresholds where only upper stages pay.*

I searched for the mechanic in multiple phrasings (not the made-up name), via live web search
(June 2026).

## Closest matches found

- **Grow a Garden (Roblox)** — https://growagarden.gg/ , https://growagardengame.com/stock ,
  https://tradekitsune.com/growagarden/calculator — the dominant "garden + market" hit
  everywhere. It is a farming game with an *in-game item economy* (shop restocks, item price
  trackers, player-to-player trading). Plants grow on **timers**, not on any market data; there
  is no real-money bet, no settlement, no regime mechanic. Theme-adjacent only; mechanically
  unrelated.
- **Vegetable Stock (board game, Arcane Wonders)** — https://www.arcanewonders.com/product/vegetable-stock/
  — harvest cards while vegetable prices fluctuate; score = holdings × final prices. A
  price-speculation card game; growth is not driven by price *character*, no regime accumulators,
  no transfer verb, no stage cliffs.
- **"Moon or Bust" oracle-synced slots** (described in
  https://www.killphilpoker.com/slot/slot-volatility-patterns-in-emerging-crypto-casino-platforms/ ,
  https://www.webopedia.com/crypto-gambling/casinos/guides/volatility-slots-live-games-crash-gambling/ )
  — crypto casinos that modulate **slot volatility** with BTC price action. The settlement is
  still a slot spin; nothing is allocated to regime types, nothing grows, nothing is grafted.
- **itch.io stock-market tags** — https://itch.io/games/tag-stock-market ,
  https://itch.io/games/genre-simulation/tag-crypto — portfolio sims and tycoon games; none found
  where live price behavior feeds typed growing organisms with a bloom-threshold payout.
- **Market-regime trading literature** (e.g. https://www.luxalgo.com/blog/market-regimes-explained-build-winning-trading-strategies/ ,
  https://arongroups.co/forex-articles/market-regime-trading/ ) — confirms regime-reading is a
  real trader skill (good for Deriv fit) but surfaced **no game** built on staking regime
  accumulators.
- **Accumulator betting** (https://www.sportsboom.co.uk/betting/accumulator-betting-explained) —
  the word collides but the mechanic doesn't: sports accas are multi-leg parlays, not
  regime-fed growth meters.

Nothing found resembling: (a) growth fed by the **statistical character** of live returns
(direction sign / realized-vol level) rather than price level or proximity; (b) a scarce,
lossy mid-round **transfer** of accrued progress between differently-typed slots; (c) settlement
through **nonlinear maturity cliffs** (one tick short of a stage pays the lower stage).

## De-skin check vs saturated genres & kept set

- Not Crash (no rising scalar, no cash-out verb, fixed bell), not Plinko/Mines/Dice/Limbo/
  Wheel/HiLo/Towers, not up/down guessing (a one-shot direction call has no allocation, no
  transfer verb, no per-slot stage settlement — and two of the four species aren't directional
  at all).
- Vs **EMBERWELL** (the nearest kept core): EMBERWELL pays leaky dwell-time of price **near a
  placed band** — pure proximity in price-space. HOTHOUSE has no bands, no placement, no
  proximity; identical growth occurs at any price level if the return character matches. The
  decision (type allocation + grafting) and the settlement (per-slot stage cliffs) both differ.
- Vs **FLUXYARD**: shares "regimes" as fuel, but FLUXYARD is reactive classification labor paid
  per correct sort; HOTHOUSE is a pre-round allocation settled by thresholds, with no labeling.
- Vs **ASSAY / RAMPART** (allocation-flavored keepers): no target-matching distance (ASSAY),
  no price-level walls eroded by touches (RAMPART). Full diff in `spec.md`.

## Verdict

**ORIGINAL.** The garden theme is common (Grow a Garden is huge, but it's an item-economy
farming game), and "volatility-aware" casino games exist, but no found game settles a real-money
stake by feeding typed organisms with the live character of market returns, with a lossy graft
verb and bloom-stage cliff payouts. Claimed only for the searches listed above.
