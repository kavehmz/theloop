# Originality check — WINDLEASE

Adversarial goal: **disprove** that "buy future time slots of a live chart at
demand-driven quotes; settle on realized price travel inside your slot" already exists as
a game. I searched the *mechanic*, not the name. All searches run 2026-06-10 via live web
search.

## Queries run

1. `betting game buy future time slots on live price chart paid by realized volatility in your time window`
2. `crypto casino game bet on how much price moves in a time interval realized range payout`
3. `"volatility" trading game auction bid on future time windows chart slots demand pricing rivals`
4. `game lease own time slots conveyor future events pay per activity in your slot itch.io`
5. `binary options "volatility" product bet price will move more than X points in next 5 seconds straddle game`
6. `"range" OR "travel" bet how far price moves not direction crypto game multiplier "doesn't matter which direction"`
7. `Deriv "stays between" "goes outside" range binary option volatility bet synthetic indices`
8. `"buy a second" OR "own a minute" of the bitcoin chart game NFT time slot ownership price action`

## Closest matches found (honest list)

- **Variance swaps** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variance_swap),
  [Carr & Lee, Realized Volatility and Variance: Options via Swaps](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~rl/OVSwithAppendices.pdf).
  The *financial ancestor* of my settlement: payoff on realized vs strike variance over a
  period. It is an OTC institutional instrument, **not a game** — no time-slot order book,
  no demand pricing, no rivals, no 5-second windows, no visual loop. WINDLEASE is to a
  variance swap roughly what Crash is to a stop-loss: a game built on a real exposure.
  This is a Deriv-fit argument, not a copy.
- **Binary options straddle strategy** — [binarytrading.com](https://www.binarytrading.com/binary-options-straddle-strategy/),
  [EBC straddle guide](https://www.ebc.com/forex/straddle-options-strategy-how-to-trade-volatility-without-direction).
  Direction-blind volatility exposure exists as a *strategy* (buy call+put). Payoff is
  from option payouts at one expiry price, not integrated tick travel; nothing about
  owning/auctioning future time intervals; not a game.
- **Deriv's own "Stays Between / Goes Outside" range contracts** —
  [Binary.com options page](https://hubert-deriv.github.io/binary-static/br_hamburger_menu_broken_mobile/en/get-started/binary-options.html),
  [Deriv synthetic indices](https://deriv.com/markets/derived-indices/synthetic-indices).
  Settles on whether the **exit price** sits inside two **price** markers — a price-region
  bet, binary payout, no travel integral, no time-slot market, no demand pricing.
- **Arbitrum TimeBoost / express-lane time auctions** —
  [TimeBoost: Do Ahead-of-Time Auctions Work?](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.18328),
  [Auctioning Time to Mitigate Latency Races](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.10094).
  Genuinely auctions future **time windows** (blockchain sequencing rights) and academic
  analysis even notes window value scales with volatility — but it is L2 infrastructure
  for arbitrageurs, not a betting game, and the buyer monetizes the window by *trading
  in it*, not by a travel-vs-quote settlement.
- **Crash / crypto-futures games** — [Tower.bet crash](https://tower.bet/crash),
  [CoinPoker crypto futures](https://coinpoker.com/crypto-futures/). Surfaced repeatedly
  for every phrasing; all are rising-multiplier cash-outs or direction calls. No overlap
  with slot ownership or travel settlement. One search engine's own summary concluded the
  described mechanic "doesn't match the standard crypto gambling games in these results."
- **Slot-machine volatility articles** ([bonus.com](https://www.bonus.com/slots/volatility/) etc.)
  and **conveyor-belt sushi incremental** ([More Sushi!, itch.io](https://pixelqube.itch.io/more-sushi))
  — keyword noise, unrelated mechanics.

## What I did NOT find

No game — crypto-casino, itch.io, app store, prediction market, or trading-gamification —
where the purchasable asset is a **future interval of the chart's time axis**, priced
dynamically by forecast volatility **and player demand**, and settled by **realized
absolute travel during the owned interval against the quote locked at purchase**. The
decision (which slice of future time, bought at which moment of its quote drift) and the
settlement (travel ÷ locked quote, capped) do not co-occur anywhere I could surface.

## Verdict

**ORIGINAL.** The settlement de-skins to "stake × realized-motion ÷ pre-agreed expected
motion" — a variance-swap-like payoff that exists in institutional finance but not as a
game; the decision de-skins to "an order book over future time slots with demand-inflated
quotes," which exists only as blockchain sequencing infrastructure, never as a chart
game. No kept core and no found product combines them. Caveats claimed honestly: I
searched the eight phrasings above on the open web; I did not exhaustively crawl app
stores or X beyond what these queries surfaced.
