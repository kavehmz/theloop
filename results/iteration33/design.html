HOW WINDLEASE WORKS

The chart is a river of wind flowing toward the NOW line. Everything to its right is the future, cut into 5-second lease windows. Every window carries a live QUOTE — the price travel the market is expected to realize inside it, estimated from the forecast haze and pushed up by demand each time anyone leases it. Click a future window to lease it at the quote of that instant. When the NOW line sweeps through your window, every tick of movement — up or down — is harvested. Beat your locked quote and you profit; a dead-calm window pays dust.

1 · READ THE SKY

The teal haze over the future is the volatility forecast: wide haze = storm expected = expensive quotes. The forecast is good but not perfect — your edge is spotting the gust it underprices, or the lull it overprices.

2 · LEASE EARLY OR LATE

Quotes drift every tick: they re-anchor to live conditions as a window approaches, and rival captains (Zephyr, Mistral, Halcyon) bid windows up by +8% per lease. A cheap window rarely stays cheap — buying early locks the bargain, buying late buys certainty.

3 · HARVEST THE TRAVEL

While your window is live, a meter fills with realized travel — every |tick move| counts, direction is irrelevant. At the window's end you're paid automatically. No cash-out, no bust: pure realized motion versus the price you paid for it.

payout = stake × min( realized_travel / locked_quote , 5.0 ) × 0.96   ·   travel = Σ |tick-to-tick move| inside your window   ·   quote = forecast travel × demand (+8% per lease)
