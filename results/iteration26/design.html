HOW TICKER FORGE WORKS

The live Volatility-100 feed closes a micro-window roughly every 1.4 seconds and mints a card. The card's rank (2–A) is the size of that window's move scaled by recent volatility — a big move forges a face card or Ace. The card's suit is the window's market regime. Cards ride the river for a few seconds; tap one to forge it into an anvil slot (tap a filled slot to scrap it). Build a 5-card hand, then FORGE-LOCK early to bank it — or ride to auto-lock hoping a better card mints. Your stake pays the hand's best synergy multiplier.
