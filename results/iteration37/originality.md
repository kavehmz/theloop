# Originality check — GAVEL (sealed-bid auctions for market-forged lots)

Goal: adversarially DISPROVE originality of the skin-free core — *sealed first-price bidding
against rival bidders for an asset whose value is realized by upcoming live price action;
settled as realized value minus the winning bid.* Searched by mechanic phrasing, not the name.

No single product combines: (a) lots whose value is a functional of the *imminent* live price path, (b) sealed first-price bidding against readable rival bidders, (c) settlement = realized value − own bid with losers unharmed.

## Verdict
**ORIGINAL.** The auction wrapper exists in board/novelty games, and chart-settled bets exist in
casino games, but their intersection — competitive sealed appraisal of short-horizon market-path
contracts with winner's-curse settlement — was not found anywhere across eight mechanic-phrased
searches. Claim limited to what was searched above (Google web via WebSearch; itch.io, Steam,
Stake, academic and prediction-market sources surfaced in results).
