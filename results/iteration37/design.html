HOW GAVEL PLAYS

Every ~30 seconds the market mints a LOT — a contract whose value will be forged by the next 15 seconds of live price action (its provenance is printed on the catalogue card: range, climb, fade, touch, or tempest). You and three rival bidders — Margin the scrooge, Volta the gambler, Kestrel the sniper — each submit one sealed bid. The hammer falls, bids are revealed, and the highest bidder buys the lot at their own price. Then the window runs live on the chart and the lot's true value realizes in front of everyone. Winner books realized value − bid; everyone else only loses the 4-credit ante.

The skill

Appraisal. Read the live volatility pips, judge what a range or a touch is really worth right now, and shade your bid against three readable rival temperaments. Bid too timid and Kestrel snipes it; bid too brave and you win a lot worth less than you paid — the winner's curse.

The dopamine

Two spikes per round: the reveal (did your sealed number top theirs?) and the realization (the value counter climbing past — or dying under — the red mark of what you paid).

The Deriv fit

Each lot is a micro exotic option on the live index — realized range, directional close, barrier touch, accumulated movement — priced not by the house but by a competitive market of bidders. Appraising volatility is the whole game.
