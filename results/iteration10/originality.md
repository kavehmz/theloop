## Honest verdict: **ORIGINAL**

Why I'm confident:
- **No game found uses a live market price as the gravitational mass of a body you orbit.**
  That coupling — price level → star mass/pull, price velocity → star drift, volatility →
  gravity flares — is the heart of APOAPSIS and appears nowhere in the results.
- The **win condition is spatial and continuous** (capture and hold a chosen orbital band across
  laps, with periodic re-trims), and the **loss is two-sided and geometric** (crash inward OR
  escape outward). Every "closest" hit is instead a *scalar rising multiplier* (Crash family) or
  a *non-wagering physics sim*. Nothing combines continuous Keplerian orbit-holding with a stake,
  a per-lap multiplier, and a fuel budget against a market-driven well.
- It is also distinct from a one-shot ballistic slingshot: the craft is in sustained flight for
  the whole round and the player actively fights orbital decay/widening tick-by-tick.
