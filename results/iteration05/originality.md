## Honest verdict: **ORIGINAL**

The bull/bear "tug-of-war" metaphor is everywhere in market commentary, and directional crypto-multiplier games and binary TOUCH/target options occupy nearby genre space — so DEADLOCK is **not** in virgin conceptual territory. But across all six phrasings I could not find an existing **game** that combines its defining features:

1. a single **contested knot** whose position is literally the **integral of live price momentum** (decaying bull/bear accumulators → a tanh settling point),
2. a bet on **displacement magnitude** via a **placed flag** (further = higher multiplier, harder to reach) rather than on direction,
3. a **PUSH** greed lever (shove the flag deeper mid-round) paired with a **travel-scaled LOCK** partial cash-out, creating a continuous press-your-luck decision while the knot is still being pulled.

That specific combination is novel. The closest single existing thing is the **binary-options TOUCH/Target bet** (early-settle on reaching a price level / betting on distance) — a financial instrument, not a tug-of-war game, and lacking the contested knot, opposing side, PUSH, and partial-LOCK mechanics.
