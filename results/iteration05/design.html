THE GAME

You don't bet which side wins. You stake how far the knot travels toward your side — and decide, mid-pull, whether to push for more or lock the slack you've earned.
01 · PLACE

Stake a flag

Pick BULL or BEAR and drag your flag along the rope. Near center is easy and pays little; out by the post is hard and pays up to 12×.

02 · PULL

Live momentum drags the knot

The knot is the settling point of real bull vs bear price momentum. A trend walks it toward a side; a reversal hauls it back through center.

03 · PUSH

Greed lever

Knot reaching your flag early? Shove the flag further out for a bigger multiplier — if your nerve and the market hold.

04 · LOCK

Bank the slack

Cash out any time for a partial scaled to how far the knot has already come. Beat the reversal, or hold for the full hit.

DEADLOCK is a design prototype. The rope is driven by a simulated momentum feed standing in for Deriv synthetic-index tick momentum & order-flow imbalance. Knot position = running settling point of two decaying momentum accumulators; rope tension = total contested force. No real funds.
