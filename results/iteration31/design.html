HOW TO READ THE WALL

01The chart is the mountain

The live Vol-75 tick stream draws the rock face in real time. Your climber rides the right edge of the route: price up is climbing, price down is sliding. Altitude is the excursion above your entry price (Base Camp) — 1.00 of price = 20 m of wall.

02Pitons are one-way ratchets

You carry exactly four. Driving one locks your current altitude as a floor, irreversibly — you can never fall below your highest piton; if price dives under it you hang from the rope until it recovers. But every spent piton shrinks your rack bonus (+12% per piton still unspent at the bell). Squalls are telegraphed 2.6 s ahead; thermals reward the brave.

03The storm settles the climb

The storm front crossing the chart is the 45 s round timer. When it reaches the climber, you're paid on the higher of your top piton or your final altitude, through a convex altitude curve — then multiplied by your rack bonus. No bust, no cash-out: a fall only ever takes you back to your last piton.
