How Fluxyard works

The market makes the items

Every tick of the live feed drops a parcel on the belt. Its glyph is the real recent price slope; its true class is the current regime. Hot tape spawns parcels faster — the belt runs at the market's pulse.

You classify, fast

Read each glyph and route it: A/← Bull, S/↓ Chop, D/→ Bear. Correct banks throughput and climbs your accuracy multiplier. A misread shatters and speeds the jam. Parcels riding off the end unsorted = overflow strikes.

Throughput, not a bust-timer

Payout = stake × tier × (correct / target) × multiplier, banked on LOCK or the third overflow strike. You're paid for correct work under accelerating pressure — not for cashing a rising number before it pops.
