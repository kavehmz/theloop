## Diff vs saturated genres and kept set

- Not Crash/Limbo (nothing climbs to a stop; no cash-out verb), not Mines/Towers/Plinko/
  Dice/HiLo/Wheel (no reveal grid, no up-down call), not a candlestick-clicker.
- vs TOLLGATE (kept): touch pays the PLAYER there; here a touch makes the player PAY —
  decision (accept/refuse mispriced premiums + cap) and settlement (income − triggered
  penalties, floored) both differ.
- vs RAMPART (kept): RAMPART spends a budget on defenses and is paid by what survives;
  SYNDICATE collects income and is settled by income minus fired claims — no allocation
  budget, opposite cash-flow direction.
- vs SPREADKEEPER (kept): no two-sided quoting, no inventory, no round-trips; one-sided
  event risk acceptance.

## Verdict

**ORIGINAL.** Insurance-themed games found are slow management/tycoon sims or data-science
contests; "house-side" mechanics found are passive bankroll staking or card-table banker
roles; every live-chart product found puts the player long the event. No existing game
was found where the player underwrites a per-round portfolio of live-chart-generated
event contingencies for premiums with a priced stop-loss cap, settled premiums-minus-claims
at a bell. Claim is limited to the searches listed above.
