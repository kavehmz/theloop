SYNDICATE — sit on the other side of the book

Every trading game makes you the punter. SYNDICATE makes you the house. Each round the market floor offers you six event-insurance contracts manufactured from the live tape — touch strikes, breakout bands, volatility doublings, 3σ candle prints, closing pins. Buyers pay you premium up front; you pay a fixed claim if their event fires before the bell.

1 · The Offer (12s)

Read the book. Each card shows the premium you collect, the claim you'd owe, the implied odds and your in-house actuary's edge read — some premiums are rich, some are toxic. Click to BIND the good risk, refuse the rest. Drag the reinsurance slider to cap your total claims for a slice of premium.

2 · On Risk (30s)

The bell rings and your bound strikes burn on the chart as gold policy lines. Now the market hunts you: every wick toward a strike heats the line and pulses the card. A touch fires the claim — red burst, ledger bleeds. A survivor turns its premium to earned gold. Past your cap, the reinsurer eats the loss.

3 · The Bell

Settlement: premiums kept − reinsurance cost − claims paid, floored at the cap you chose. Reserves compound round over round. A fresh mispriced book lands in seconds — underwrite one more.

Why this is a Deriv game

Touch/no-touch, range breaks, volatility events — these are Deriv's own contracts, seen from the desk that prices them. SYNDICATE teaches premium vs. probability, edge identification, tail risk and hedging — and the whole experience hangs off a live market chart where the suspense is inverted: you root against the touch.
