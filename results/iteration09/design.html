How BALLAST works

The live market is not a number on a chart — it is a physical force. Price velocity tips the beam left and right; volatility hits it as quakes. Your job is structural engineering under fire: counterweight the lean, keep the beam near level, and stack as high as your nerve allows.

01 · STAKE

Set your bet & build

Pick a stake and press BUILD. A beam balanced on a single pivot appears — and the market immediately starts leaning it.

02 · COUNTERWEIGHT

Place mass with intent

Slide the glowing block along the beam. Torque = mass × distance from the pivot, so far-out drops are powerful but unforgiving. Drop on the high side to level out.

03 · CLIMB

Every block = more height = more multiplier

Survive and your multiplier grows. But each block raises the center of mass, so the same market tilt swings a tall stack far harder.

04 · BANK or TOPPLE

Lock it in — or lose it

Cash out anytime to bank stake × multiplier. Let the beam tilt past 24° and the whole stack slides off. No bust RNG — only balance you defend.
