HOW IT WORKS ↓

THE GAME

You don't bet which way the market goes. You harvest how hard it shakes — and cage it before it blows.
1

Arm the field

Set the magnetic containment field width. Tight fields multiply your energy yield but rupture under pressure. Place your stake.

2

Ignite

The live price becomes a plasma filament inside the ring. Every tick of movement — up or down, doesn't matter — pumps the reactor.

3

Harvest the chop

Realized volatility fills your Energy meter. A wild market is a torrent. But the same turbulence raises Breach Pressure toward 100%.

4

Eject & bank

Cash out anytime: stake × energy × field multiplier. Wait for more power, but a breach at 100% detonates the round and your stake.

READING THE REACTOR

Plasma intensity = realized volatility

The filament swells and roars in proportion to how violently the market is moving right now. Calm = thin trickle. Storm = thick, lashing arc.

Breach pressure = danger

Sustained excursions past your field band and sharp volatility spikes load the ring. The outer glow reddens and pulses faster as you near rupture.

The containment ring

Two faint guide arcs mark your field band. When plasma presses them they spark — a live read on how close you are to losing containment.

The eject decision

One dial, one exit. Bank early for safe, modest energy — or ride a screaming market for a huge multiplier and dare the ring to hold.
