How OVERTONE pays

01 · LISTEN

The market hums

The live price is band-passed to expose its dominant micro-cycle — a real frequency and amplitude that drift as volatility shifts. Calm markets hum slow and quiet; wild markets scream fast and loud.

02 · TUNE

Match three dials

Steer FREQ, PHASE and GAIN to lay your amber wave over the market's cyan wave. Coherence = how tightly the two oscillators are locked, instant by instant.

03 · RESONATE

Hold the lock

Above the lock threshold your Resonance Q compounds — and it climbs faster in wilder markets. Drift out of phase and Q decays; flatline below dropout and the tone snaps.

Bank stake × Q
