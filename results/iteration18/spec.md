## Name & pitch
**OVERTONE** — *Tune your tone to the market's hum. The longer you stay in resonance, the louder you're paid.*

## Core loop (second-to-second)
1. The live price, de-trended, is shown as a glowing oscillation. The market always "hums" — micro-cycles in volatility produce a dominant frequency and amplitude that drift continuously.
2. You own a **tuning fork** with three live dials:
   - **FREQ** (how fast your wave cycles)
   - **PHASE** (where your wave's crest sits in time)
   - **GAIN** (your wave's amplitude)
   You drag the dials (or use the ◀▶ nudges / arrow keys) to lay YOUR sine wave over the price's oscillation.
3. A **coherence meter** reads, instant by instant, how tightly your wave is locked to the price's wave (frequency match × phase alignment × amplitude proximity). Above the **lock threshold** you are "in resonance": the screen blooms, the tone swells, and your **Resonance Q** climbs — and Q *compounds* the longer you hold lock.
4. The market drifts: its frequency wanders, it changes amplitude, it sometimes jumps key. You must keep re-tuning to chase the drift and stay locked. Drift out of phase and Q **decays** (fast if you're badly off, slow if you're close).
5. Your live payout = **stake × Q**. At any moment you may **RELEASE the tone** to bank stake × current Q. If coherence collapses below a **dropout floor** for too long, the tone **snaps** and the round ends — banking only a small consolation of the Q you'd locked in, never the peak. So: ride the resonance to grow Q, but release before the drift breaks your lock.

## How the live market drives it
The price's oscillation is **intrinsic market behaviour**: short-horizon returns are band-passed to expose the market's dominant micro-cycle — its real instantaneous frequency and amplitude. Volatility regime changes literally change the pitch you must chase: a calm market hums slow and quiet (easy to lock, low ceiling); a volatile market screams fast and loud (hard to lock, huge Q ceiling). Phase coherence is computed from the *actual* price wave vs. your dialed wave — nothing is faked. When the market trends, the carrier drifts and your phase slips; you feel the market's character through your fingertips on the dials.

## The bet & payout
- **Stake** up front. Live payout = **stake × Resonance Q**.
- **Q** starts at 1.00, rises while coherence > lock threshold (rate scales with *how tight* the lock and the market's amplitude/frequency — wilder markets pay steeper), decays while below it, and **snaps** (round over, consolation only) if you flatline below the dropout floor.
- **RELEASE** banks stake × Q instantly. The skill is reading the drift and releasing at the crest of your own lock — greed costs you the snap.
- No direction guess, no touch, no spatial band, no rising-scalar-bust: outcome is the *quality and duration of a continuous match*.

## Why it's addictive
- **Tactile flow state.** Three analog dials and a swelling tone make tuning feel like finding a radio station out of static — the moment of lock is a physical "click" of dopamine.
- **Compounding tension.** Q growth accelerates the longer you hold, so every extra second is worth more — but the drift is always trying to break you. Classic "one more second… release NOW" pull.
- **Skill expression.** Anticipating the market's frequency drift (leading the dial instead of chasing) separates novices from masters; the ceiling is high but the first lock is learnable in seconds.
- **Sensory reward.** Lock blooms the whole screen into harmonic glow and overtone particles; dropout shatters into dissonant static. You *feel* every win and loss.
