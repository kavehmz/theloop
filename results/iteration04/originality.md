## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**

I could not find any existing game that fuses the three load-bearing pieces of SLINGSHOT VEGA:
1. **Charge = ∫|price velocity| dt** with a *player-timed release* (active read of the live tape),
2. a committed **ballistic arc whose range is set by stored power and bent in real time by SIGNED
   live price drift as crosswind/lift**, and
3. a bet on **spatial distance reached on a gate/strike ladder**, with a mid-flight catch-or-hold.

The closest existing things are Aviator/Crash-style "flying object + cash-out" games and
crypto-futures cash-out games — both are **scalar, RNG-or-direction bets** (and Aviator/Crash is a
banned genre). They lack the charge-from-momentum, the physical arc, the live price-as-wind, and the
distance-gate ladder. Name collisions ("Slingshot Crash", SLING token, Slingshot Finance) are
unrelated. No near-identical game was found.
