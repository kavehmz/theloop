# CAUSTIC — adversarial originality check

**Goal:** disprove the originality of the core mechanic — *the live price path acts as a
specular mirror; the player commits position+angle of a few fixed light emitters; settlement
is which fixed-odds boundary zones the multi-bounce reflected rays terminate in at the bell.*

Searches run 2026-06-10 via web search, phrased around the *mechanic* (not the name).

## Queries & closest matches

### 1. `trading game price chart as mirror reflect light beams bet`
- [Bulkowski on Price Mirrors](https://thepatternsite.com/Mirrors.html) — "price mirror" is a
  technical-analysis *pattern symmetry* concept (price retracing its own shape). No light, no
  geometry, no game. Not a match.
- [CoinPoker Crypto Futures](https://coinpoker.com/crypto-futures/) — "mirrors real-world
  volatility" = simulation fidelity, not a mirror mechanic. Not a match.
- [Light Rays | Math Playground](https://www.mathplayground.com/logic_light_rays.html) — a
  classic mirror-placement puzzle: *the player places/rotates mirrors* to route a beam through
  a static authored level. In CAUSTIC the inverse holds — the mirror is the *market* (unknown,
  evolving, uncontrollable) and the player only owns the emitters. No betting, no live data.
- [ChartGame](https://chartgame.com/), [Stock Market Game](https://www.stockmarketgame.org/) —
  chart trading simulators, no optics. Not a match.

### 5. `bet where ball bounces off stock price line lands gambling arcade fintech`
- [Plinko (BGaming)](https://bgaming.com/games/plinko), [Plinko at Stake](https://stake.com/casino/games/plinko),
  [Monster Drop](https://www.betson.com/amusement-products/monster-drop/) — the nearest
  *settlement* cousin: an object bounces and the landing slot pays. But Plinko's bounce field
  is a fixed peg lattice, the outcome is pure chance, the player makes **no geometric
  decision** (no aim, no position), and there is **no market surface**. CAUSTIC's bounce
  surface IS the live price path and the entire skill is committing aim against its likely
  future shape; reflections are deterministic given the realized path. Decision differs
  (aim/position vs. none), surface differs (live market vs. static pegs), and the
  one-bounce-to-many mapping off slope/curvature has no Plinko analogue.
- ["Playing the Bounce" (ProRightLine)](https://prorightline.com/playing-bounce/) — a trading
  metaphor about price bouncing off moving averages. Not a game.

## Nearest-neighbor summary
| Neighbor | Shares | Differs |
|---|---|---|
| Laser/mirror puzzles (Laser Maze, Light Rays, Laser Nodes) | specular reflection as a verb | player owns the mirrors, surface static & known, no stake, no odds, no market |
| Plinko | "thing bounces, landing slot pays" | no player aim, no market surface, pure-chance peg field, no convergence bonus |
| Crash / multiplier games | wager + bell cadence | no rising scalar, no cash-out in CAUSTIC |
| WAKELINE-style path prediction (in-set) | future path matters | CAUSTIC never scores path similarity; it scores the *image of committed rays under reflection* |

## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**
Across six mechanic-phrased searches (optics, lasers, mirrors, ricochet, Plinko-adjacent,
ray-tracing) no existing game was found in which a **live market price path is the reflective
surface** and the wager is **committed emitter geometry settled by where multi-bounce
reflections land in odds zones**. The two nearest families (laser puzzles; Plinko) each miss
at least two of the three pillars: live-market surface, committed aim as the bet, zone
settlement of reflected rays. Claimed only for the searches recorded above.
