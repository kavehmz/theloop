The market is your mirror.

CAUSTIC turns the live price path into a liquid-mercury mirror. You command three light cannons on the rail above the chart: slide them, set their angle, and commit. For sixteen seconds the market keeps re-forging the mirror beneath your beams — every tick bends your light somewhere new. At the bell, you're paid by which odds-zones your reflected rays are illuminating.

01 · AIM — 6s

Place your light

Drag each cannon along the rail and tilt its beam with the diamond handle. A live preview shows where each ray would land off the current curve — but the curve won't stay still.

02 · FLIGHT — 16s

The mirror deforms

Beams are locked. The price keeps redrawing the surface: a steep candle under a strike point whips its reflection across half the board. The "IF BELL NOW" ticker breathes with every tick.

03 · BELL

Frozen light pays

The frame freezes; each ray's landing zone pays its multiplier on a third of your stake. Two rays converging in one zone pay ×1.5; all three is a ×3 CAUSTIC FOCUS on everything.
