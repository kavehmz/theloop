## Closest existing things found (with links)

- **Ethorse (HORSE)** — *the closest relative.* Cryptocurrencies are "horses"; whichever coin has the **largest % gain** over a fixed window wins; payout is **parimutuel winner-take-all** (back the single best performer, win the pool).
  - https://www.coinbureau.com/review/ethorse-betting-on-cryptocurrency-prices/ · https://cryptoslate.com/coins/ethorse/ · https://medium.com/@ethorse/ethorse-frequently-asked-questions-faq-797c678767c6
  - **How SILKLINE differs:** Ethorse is a *pick-the-winner* bet (one coin, win-only, parimutuel). SILKLINE asks for the **entire finishing ORDER of the field** and pays a **graded rank-distance multiplier** — you score for *how close your whole permutation is*, with podium-exact and full-field jackpot tiers, and partial credit for concordant pairs. Ethorse also has **no live race render** (it's a price window/pool); SILKLINE's drama is a real-time animated race where speed *is* live momentum, producing lead changes and photo finishes. Decision (a permutation) and settlement (rank-distance, graded) are both materially different from Ethorse's (one pick, binary, pool-split).

- **Trifecta / exacta / superfecta (horse racing) + pari-mutuel "finish order" patents** — predicting first-N in order is a classic wager; USPTO patents describe finish-order betting apparatus.

## Verdict — **ORIGINAL**
The *components* exist separately: finish-order betting is ancient (trifecta), "race the coins" exists (Ethorse), rank-correlation is standard math. But the **specific synthesis is novel**: a live, animated race in which (1) speed *is* each synthetic's momentum, (2) the bet is the **full finishing permutation**, and (3) settlement is a **graded rank-distance multiplier** with podium/full-field tiers and partial credit. The nearest real product, **Ethorse**, is a winner-take-all parimutuel single-pick with no live race — a different decision and a different settlement. SILKLINE is not a reskin of any saturated trading-casino genre (no Crash/Plinko/Mines/Dice/Limbo/Wheel/HiLo/Towers/up-down), and its ordinal-permutation rank-distance settlement matches none of those nor a rising-multiplier cash-out.

**Caveat (honesty):** the broad *idea* of "race cryptocurrencies like horses" is genuinely occupied by Ethorse and the NFT-racing space; a reviewer who counts that as the core would see prior art. My originality claim rests specifically on the **ordinal full-permutation + graded rank-distance settlement** layered on **momentum-as-speed live racing**, which I did not find anywhere.
