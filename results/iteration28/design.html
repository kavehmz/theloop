How SILKLINE runs

Six live Deriv synthetic indices line up as six silks. You don't bet up or down — you call the finishing order of the whole field, then watch them trade places. Each runner's speed is its index's live momentum, so the lead really changes and the wire is really a photo finish.

1 · Read the form

Each silk carries a live synthetic — Vol 75, Boom 500, Crash 1000, Step, Jump 100, Range Break. The form line shows trailing momentum. Drag the cards into the order you think they'll finish.

2 · The gun

Every tick, a runner advances by its index's signed momentum. A market ripping upward surges to the rail; one rolling over gets swallowed. Positions are cumulative — late charges and stumbles are real.

3 · Settle by rank-distance

At the wire your predicted order is compared to the realized order. More correctly-ordered pairs = bigger multiplier; exact podium and exact full field pay top-heavy bonuses. Wrong-everywhere pays zero.
