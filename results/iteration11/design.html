▼ how it works

STARFORGE — the live price draws your figure

Pick a constellation and a fixed pattern of stars materializes across the chart. The glowing comet is the live market price. As the price path sweeps through each star's capture ring in sequence, a ley-line snaps into place and the figure takes shape. Every star is decaying — if one burns out before the comet reaches it, the figure breaks. The market itself decides whether the price can swing far enough, fast enough, to connect them all.

READ THE SKY

Volatility scatters the stars. Calm markets cluster them near the price rail — easy threads. Wild markets fling them wide, demanding real swings to connect. The same template is harder when the market is hot.

BANK OR PUSH

The instant you light enough stars for a partial figure, you can lock that payout — or push for the remaining stars and the full rarity multiplier. A draining lifespan on the final star is the most dangerous, most delicious moment in the game.

COLLECT THE COSMOS

Completed figures fill your Almanac and stack a streak bonus that supercharges your next multiplier. Rare constellations are trophies — chase the Mythic loop and watch the whole sky flare.
