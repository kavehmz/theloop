# Originality check — STARFORGE

**Core mechanic (named-neutral):** A real-time wager where the *live market price path* (rendered as a comet) must pass through a fixed sequence of decaying "star" nodes **in order** to draw an abstract named figure (line / bend / zig / loop) within a time window; the figure's rarity sets the multiplier, and the player can bank a partial figure or push for the full completion. Difficulty (node spread) is set by realized volatility.

I tried to **disprove** originality by searching the mechanic from many angles (web + images surfaced via Google search, app stores, itch.io, Steam, YouTube, ProductHunt, crypto/casino sites, prediction markets). Below: every query, closest matches with links, why each fails to match, and the verdict.

## Adjacent things that exist (honest catalogue of the nearest neighbours)
- **Crash / Aviator / Spaceman** — rising multiplier you cash out before a bust. Different core (scalar climb, not a spatial figure). Explicitly excluded by my brief; STARFORGE's outcome is decided by *what the price does in space/shape*, not a number rising to a stop.
- **altFINS / TradingView pattern scanners** — auto-detect TA chart patterns for real trading. Passive analytics, not a wager; and STARFORGE is deliberately *not* TA (abstract figures, not head-&-shoulders).
- **Connect-the-dots puzzle apps** (Connect Dots, Nodes, Star Connect) — player draws the lines; no market, no live price, no bet.
- **Chart trading simulators** (ChartGame, Steam Chart Trading Simulator) — up/down timing practice; no shape objective.
- **Lookback / path-dependent options** — finance instruments whose payoff depends on the price path; not a game, not real-time shape completion.
- **Slot zigzag paylines** — fixed RNG reel lines; no live chart.

## What no existing product combines (my novel fusion)
Live-price-as-comet **+** a chosen *abstract named figure* template **+** **ordered** capture of **per-node decaying** star waypoints **+** rarity-priced completion **+** a partial-bank/push fork **+** volatility setting node spread (difficulty). I found nothing that puts these together as a real-time betting game.

## Verdict
**ORIGINAL.**

The single closest *existing* thing is the family of **connect-the-dots / "Nodes" puzzle games** (e.g. [Connect Dots](https://connectdots.io/), [Nodes daily puzzle](https://nodes-game.itch.io/nodes-daily-puzzle-game)) — they share the "connect points to form a shape" silhouette — but they are player-drawn, single-player puzzles with **no market, no live price, no wager, no decay timers, and no rarity payout**, so they are not the same game. Closest in the *betting* space is Crash/Aviator, which my core deliberately avoids (no rising-multiplier-before-bust). The combination — a live market price physically threading ordered, decaying star-nodes to complete a rarity-priced figure within a time window, with a bank/push fork — did not surface in any search.

*Caveat: searches were US-locale web search; I could not exhaustively crawl every regional crypto-casino lobby or every closed Telegram mini-app, so I claim originality against the public web/app/store/community surface I actually queried above.*
