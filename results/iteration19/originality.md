# STOWLINE — Originality check (adversarial)

**Core mechanic under test (skin-free):** the player rotates/places shaped pieces — whose
size/shape/colour are *forged from live price action* — into a bounded vault, and the wager
is settled by **packed coverage (% area filled)** reached at the moment a piece can no longer
fit and forces a spill. I tried to DISPROVE its originality by searching the mechanic, not the name.

## Queries run (Google web + the platforms each surfaces: app stores, itch.io, Steam, Poki, Reddit, patents, crypto-game sites)
1. `trading game pieces forged from price action pack into container density bet`
2. `tetris polyomino packing game driven by live stock crypto price chart wager`
3. `cargo stowage hex grid puzzle volatility generated blocks fit rotate fintech game`
4. `bin packing game market volatility creates pieces cash out coverage percentage multiplier`
5. `Deriv synthetic index game tetris stacking blocks options trading arcade`
6. `candlestick becomes tetris block stack the candles game crypto`
7. `"pack the vault" OR "stow cargo" gambling game multiplier price feed pieces`

(X/Twitter and YouTube were probed via the same phrasings through Google's web+video index; no
dedicated result surfaced beyond the items below.)

## Closest matches found, with links + why each is NOT this game
- **"Tetris" (TETRIS) crypto token** — https://coinmarketcap.com/currencies/tetris-2/ . A PvP wallet-connect
  dapp where you stake ETH and play *classic* Tetris against opponents. Pieces are the standard 7
  tetrominoes; nothing is generated by a price feed and there is no coverage-density settlement. It is
  "bet on a Tetris match," not "price-forged packing." Different core.
- **Hexa Grid / Hex Blocks / Hex Puzzle (mobile)** — e.g. https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.simplefishgames.hexagonpuzzle
  and https://www.coolmathgames.com/0-hex-blocks . Pure hex-fit puzzles with random offline pieces, no money,
  no market, no spill-wager. Confirms hex packing exists as a genre but not tied to a live financial feed or a bet.
- **Stacktris (Poki)** — https://poki.com/en/g/stacktris . Tetrominoes stacked under *physics/balance* (a BALLAST-like
  topple loss), free-to-play, no price feed, no coverage payout. Different loss condition and no market driver.
- **"Hot Wax" (Steam)** — https://store.steampowered.com/app/3041750/Hot_Wax/ . A falling-candle puzzler — "candle"
  here is wax, not a candlestick chart. Zero financial connection. Pure coincidence of the word "candle."
- **US Patent 12,424,055 "Secure participation wagering system using bin packing to facilitate digital games"** —
  https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/12424055 . Closest *conceptual* hit. But "bin
  packing" here is an internal **algorithm to lay out a wagering board / distribute outcomes**, and "volatility"
  is gambling payout-variance jargon — NOT a live market feed forging the player's pieces, and the player is not
  performing spatial tessellation paid by coverage. Scope differs at the core (algorithmic layout vs. player-skill
  packing of market-generated geometry).
- **Deriv synthetic indices** — https://deriv.com/markets/synthetic/ . The target platform's instruments (incl.
  Crash/Boom). Confirms the live-feed substrate exists; no packing game is built on it.
- **Tetris-as-blockchain metaphor articles** (Medium) — these are explainer essays, not games.

## Verdict: ORIGINAL
No existing product was found that fuses all three pillars: (1) pieces whose geometry/colour are
**intrinsically forged from real price volatility/direction**, (2) a **spatial tessellation/packing**
skill loop, and (3) a real-money wager **settled by packed coverage density** with a press-your-luck
bank-vs-spill decision. Hex/Tetris packing puzzles exist but are offline and unmonetised; vault/Tetris
"crypto" products are either reskinned slots or classic-Tetris betting; the one bin-packing wagering
patent uses packing as a backend layout algorithm, not as the player's market-driven action. The
combination is, on this search, novel — and it is also distinct from every kept core (it is fit-and-density,
not corridor-drawing, energy-banking, territory-share, balance/topple, classification, or routing).
