Welcome to TOLLGATE

The price is a comet on a road. To its right stands a ladder of tollgates — future price levels you can own. Buy a gate cheap. Rent on it climbs as the price drives toward it. Then choose: hold for the price to crash through and pay the full toll, or flip your claim for the risen rent. Far gates pay huge — if the price stretches to reach them.

SCROLL FOR HOW IT WORKS ↓

MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE, PLAYABLE

Own the levels the price is about to visit.

01 · BID

Buy a future level

Each gate is a discrete price level with a live rent (bid price) and a toll multiplier. Near gates are cheap and pay small; far gates cost more in expectation and pay big — because the price has to stretch to reach them.

02 · DEMAND

Rent moves with the price

As the live comet drifts toward a gate, rival bidders pile in and its rent inflates in real time — driven by distance and momentum, not RNG. Buy before the wave; the gate you grabbed cheap turns hot.

03 · COLLECT

Touch, or flip

Touch: the price reaches your gate's lane → it detonates and pays the full toll. Flip: sell your claim into the inflamed market for its risen rent, banking the spread before the touch ever happens.

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Not up/down. Not a crash multiplier.

There's no single number climbing to a bust. Outcomes are decided by whether the price path spatially reaches the levels you bought — and by a live bid you can sell into. It's the order book, turned into a board you can play.

DERIV FIT

Strikes & barriers, gamified

It mirrors real strike/barrier instruments and order-book pricing: bids that inflate with demand, mark-to-market exits, payoffs on a level being touched. The exact intuition a Deriv trader lives in — as a one-tap arcade game.

THE LOOP

Two exits, zero idle

Every owned gate is a live question: ride for the toll or flip the spread? The rent ticker never stops, the comet never stops, and the near-miss — price reversing one lane short — keeps you reaching for one more round.
