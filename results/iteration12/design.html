How COREBORE works

01 · SINK

The bit descends

Your tungsten drill head sinks continuously down a living rock face. You steer left/right; depth is driven by how far the live market price travels.

02 · CARVE

Price draws the gold

Each tick carves a new rock slice. The price's level sets where the ore vein sits laterally; sustained trends lay rich mother-lode gold, chop lays low-grade scatter.

03 · EXTRACT

Dwell on the seam

Overlap a vein and DWELL — the extraction beam pumps ore into your hopper. Richer seams and longer dwell fill it faster, but burn descent budget and let pressure build.

04 · WINCH

Bank before the cave-in

Volatility fills shaft pressure and spawns gas pockets. WINCH to bank stake × depth-multiplier × hopper-fill. Push deeper for more — but a cave-in buries everything un-winched.
