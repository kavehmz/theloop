## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**

No existing product combines the load-bearing pillars of COREBORE:
1. **The live price carves the lateral position and grade of ore seams** in real time (price level → seam X; trend persistence → mother-lode vs. scatter).
2. **2D spatial pursuit-extraction** — you steer a continuously descending bit to intersect those seams and DWELL to mine; two players on the same feed earn different amounts by spatial skill.
3. **Realized volatility → shaft pressure + gas/fault/flood hazards** gating a depth-multiplier.
4. **WINCH-or-PUSH** decision banking stake × depth-multiplier × hopper-fill, with cave-in forfeiting un-winched ore.

Each pillar exists somewhere in isolation (arcade diggers; mining slots; crash cash-outs; chart games), but the **combination — a market-chart-driven excavation skill game with spatial extraction and volatility hazards — returned no near-identical match** across all eight phrasings, including two where the search engine itself stated the described game does not exist. It is also distinct from every kept design (it is active depth-extraction, not TOLLGATE's passive level-ownership; not a Mines grid reveal; not a Crash scalar climb; not SLIPSTREAM's rail-hugging survival).
