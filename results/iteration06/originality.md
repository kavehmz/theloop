# Originality check — SLIPSTREAM

**Method:** adversarial. I tried to *disprove* originality by describing the CORE MECHANIC
(not the made-up name) in many phrasings, across Google web, itch.io, app stores, crypto/
casino game sites, trading-tool sites, and Reddit/YouTube-scoped queries. Date: 2026-06-09.
Tooling note: I could not log into X in this shared-Chrome session and did not want to
rabbit-hole, so the X/Twitter pass is **not claimed** below — I only claim what I actually ran.

## Core mechanic being tested
> Pilot an avatar through a **two-walled canyon whose walls are the live market's volatility
> bands** (ceiling = upper band, floor = lower band); the gap **breathes with realized
> volatility** (widens when vol rises, pinches when vol contracts). Hazard = wall collision.
> The live **price line is the mid-rail**, and it is the *most rewarding and most lethal* line:
> a "slipstream" payout multiplier accelerates the closer you fly to it, and collectible
> crystals spawn pinned to it. You ride a continuously-rising multiplier and **cash out**
> before a volatility crush pinches the canyon shut on you.

## Closest existing thing (single)
**Crash-family avatar games — Aviator / Pilot Chickens / Shuffle's Chicken.**
Link: https://spribe.co/games/aviator
Why it's the closest: shares the *betting frame* (rising multiplier + cash-out-before-bust)
and an avatar skin. That is where the resemblance ends.

## Why SLIPSTREAM is NOT a copycat of it
- **Bust source:** Aviator busts on a hidden RNG. SLIPSTREAM busts on a **physical collision
  with a live-data wall** — the player's own piloting and the market's volatility decide death,
  not a roll.
- **Terrain:** Aviator has no terrain. SLIPSTREAM's obstacle course is a **two-walled
  volatility envelope** (bands), continuously reshaped tick-by-tick by realized volatility —
  the difficulty literally *is* the market's mood (widen on high vol, pinch on crush).
- **Inverted reward gradient:** Aviator's reward is uniform (just wait). SLIPSTREAM pins the
  reward to the **price line itself** via slipstream acceleration + rail-bound crystals, so the
  optimal path is the *most dangerous* path. That coupling of "survival = volatility width,
  reward = price proximity, forced end = volatility crush" is the novel core, and I found it
  nowhere.
- **Skill axis:** Aviator is a timing guess. SLIPSTREAM is continuous spatial piloting with a
  read on volatility regime.

## Verdict
**ORIGINAL.** No near-identical game found. The nearest betting relative (crash/Aviator) shares
only the multiplier-and-cash-out wrapper; the nearest *piloting* relatives (tunnel flyers) have
zero market coupling; and the band-canyon concept exists only as serious trading indicators, not
as a game. The fusion — a bettable volatility-canyon flight where you flirt with the live price
line for reward while the bands try to crush you — appears genuinely new.

*Honesty caveats:* searches were US-region Google + the sites above; I did not complete an
X/Twitter logged-in pass or an exhaustive non-English / Telegram-mini-app sweep, so I claim
originality against everything I actually searched, not against literally all of the internet.
