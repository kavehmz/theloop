How Emberwatch works

Six markets, six clocks

Each coal is bound to a different Deriv synthetic index. Its cooling rate is driven live by that market's volatility — a violent index burns down fast, a calm one barely fades. The six clocks constantly diverge.

One pair of bellows

You hold a single breath that recharges over a couple of seconds. You can never save them all at once — you triage, reviving whichever coal is closest to ashing out, always a beat behind.

Bank, then seal

Every second alive, the summed heat trickles into your hearth pot, faster as you cross survival tiers. Seal the hearth to bank the live pot — or let the first coal die and keep only your locked tier.

Skin-free core: your bet is how to divide one scarce, recharging intervention budget across N independent objects that each decay toward death on their own live-signal-driven clock — settled by how many are still alive when the FIRST one dies × the survival-time tier the whole set reached. Parallel sustain under a shared budget — distinct from any single-object trading loop.
