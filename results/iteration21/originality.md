## Verdict: **ORIGINAL**

No existing game — in the crypto/trading-casino space, on itch.io/Steam, in arcade keep-aloft titles, or in sports-betting formats — combines:
1. **Several objects sustained in parallel,** each decaying on its **own clock driven by a different live market signal** (volatility/shock per synthetic index);
2. a **single scarce, recharging intervention budget** the player must **triage** across them (the bet IS the allocation of attention); and
3. settlement by **count-alive-at-first-death × survival-time tier**, with an all-set bonus.

The saturated trading-casino genre is uniformly **single-object** (one multiplier, one pilot, one meter). Keep-aloft/plate-spinning exists only as reflex arcade or as a management *metaphor* — never wired to live markets with a wager. The settlement rule is structurally distinct from cash-out, parlay, and survivor-pool formats. The mechanic is genuinely new; the Deriv synthetic-index family is the natural (and so far unused) substrate for it.

**Honest caveat:** "keep N things alive" is an old arcade *feeling*, and "divide your attention" reflex games exist. The originality is not the sensation but the **decision+settlement loop**: parallel live-signal-driven decay under one shared triage budget, paid by survivors-at-first-failure. That specific loop returned zero matches.
