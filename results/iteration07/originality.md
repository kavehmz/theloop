# PULSEPOINT — Adversarial Originality Check

**Core mechanic under test (not the name):** A rhythm/timing game where the *beat* is the live market's own price reversals (momentum zero-crossings / local tops & bottoms). The player taps at the instant price turns; timing error is graded PERFECT/GOOD/OK/MISS, consecutive on-beat hits build a Flow multiplier (combo chain), and the payout is the accuracy distribution across a phrase — not direction guessing, not a target/grid touch, not a rising-multiplier cash-out.

## Closest matches found (with links) and why each differs

### 1. BC.GAME — "Tap Trading" (Sept 2025) — CLOSEST NAMED NEIGHBOR
- https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/bcgame-launches-tap-trading-a-price-touch-multiplier-game-for-crypto-markets-302562737.html
- https://www.businessofigaming.com/bc-game-tap-trading/
- **What it actually is (verified by fetching the release):** you tap a **green target block on a right-hand grid**; each block shows a multiplier and a time window; if the live price *touches* that block before the timer expires you win stake×multiplier, else you lose the stake. The CEO frames it as expressing a view on "direction, magnitude and time."
- **Why it's NOT my mechanic:** This is a **price-touch-a-target-cell** bet (a GridGame/touch-option cousin) — outcome decided by *whether price reaches a level in a window*. There is **no timing-accuracy grading, no rhythm/beat, no combo chain off price reversals, no flow**. Despite the shared word "tap," it's a different family entirely (and "Tap Trading" / taptradings.com is a white-label grid binary-option product).

## Honest verdict: **ORIGINAL**

Across 8 phrasings on web, images, crypto-casino sites, app stores, itch.io, ProductHunt-adjacent listings, Reddit, and YouTube, I found **no game whose core loop is "tap precisely when the live price reverses, graded by timing error, chained into a flow/combo multiplier, with the payout decided by accuracy across a phrase."**

- The nearest *named* thing, BC.GAME "Tap Trading," shares only the word "tap" — it is a touch-a-target-cell multiplier bet (GridGame family), not rhythm/timing. (Verified by reading its press release.)
- Stock-market *music* exists, but as non-interactive art, never as a staked timing game.
- Rhythm games exist, but their beat is an authored song, never a live market feed; and they are not betting products.

**Why I'm confident it's not a banned/saturated reskin:** it is not up/down guessing (direction is irrelevant — only the *moment* of the turn scores), not a candlestick-clicker (you act on continuous momentum zero-crossings, not discrete bars), not Crash/Aviator (a sloppy phrase still pays; value comes from the accuracy *distribution*, not a scalar climbing until it busts), and not Plinko/Mines/Dice/Limbo/Wheel/HiLo/Towers. The novel fusion — **market-reversal microstructure rendered as a playable, comboable rhythm track with skill-graded payouts** — appears unoccupied.
