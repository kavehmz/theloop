HOW PULSEPOINT WORKS

01 · THE BEAT

The market keeps time

Price velocity is smoothed into a momentum oscillator. Every zero-crossing is a real local top or bottom — a beat. Choppy markets stutter; trends fall silent then snap.

02 · THE TAP

Lock the turn

A reticle contracts toward the predicted reversal. Tap on the turn. Your error is graded PERFECT / GOOD / OK / MISS — no direction guessing, only the moment.

03 · THE FLOW

Chain it

Unbroken on-beat hits braid a Flow multiplier and ramp the heat. A miss resets Flow to ×1 but never busts you — the phrase plays on.
