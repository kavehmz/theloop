## Honest verdict: **ORIGINAL**

Across web + surfaced Reddit/Pinside/Steam/itch.io/app results, I found:
- pinball games **themed** around stocks (no live data, no chain-bet),
- a forex **strategy** called "Momentum Pinball" (no game),
- generic combo-streak skill games like Flipside (no market, no bet),
- generic paddle/breakout/Pong clones (no market, no bet).

**None** combines all four load-bearing pillars of RICOCHET: (1) a ball whose speed, table tilt and bumper energy are **continuous functions of a live price feed**, (2) **flipper-deflection skill** to keep it in play, (3) a **reset-on-miss (not bank, not bust) combo-chain**, and (4) a **financial bet settled by the peak chain multiplier**. The combination appears unoccupied to the depth I searched.

**Caveats / limits of this check:** searches were English-language and US-biased via WebSearch; I did not directly browse X, YouTube, ProductHunt, or the iOS/Play stores through their native apps (WebSearch surfaced itch.io and app-store-adjacent pages but I did not crawl every storefront). A niche, recently-launched, or non-English title could exist outside these results. The risk is low given the specificity of the four-pillar combination, but it is not zero.
