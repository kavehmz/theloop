HOW RICOCHET WORKS

The market throws the ball

Ball speed and launch power track price velocity. The whole table tilts with trend — up-trends feed the rotors, down-trends drag toward the drain. Volatility spins the rotors hotter, so wild markets offer the biggest chains.

Keep the chain alive

Every clean rotor hit links your combo-chain and ramps the multiplier. Let the rhythm lapse without scoring and the chain resets to x1 — it never banks, it never busts. You are racing your own best run.

Settle on your peak

No cash-out button. When the ball drains, you're paid stake × the highest chain you held. Reading volatility and trend literally makes you play better — the chart skill is the game skill.

Skin-free core: your bet is the timing of paddle-strikes that deflect a live-price-driven ball through scoring rotors to hold an unbroken combo-chain, settled by the peak chain multiplier reached when the ball drains.
