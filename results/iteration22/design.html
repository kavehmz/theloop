How SpreadKeeper works

You're the market maker. You don't bet on direction — you post a two-sided quote around the live price and earn the spread every time the market oscillates across it. The same chart that pays you can kill you: a strong trend fills only one side, loads toxic inventory, and bleeds you toward the risk limit.

01 · POST

Quote a bid & an ask

Set spread width, directional skew, and size per fill. Tight quotes capture more per round-trip but accumulate inventory faster. The shaded band on the chart is your live quote.

02 · CAPTURE

Round-trips pay

Price dips to your bid → you buy. Price rises to your ask → you sell. A buy answered by a sell is one round-trip: you bank the full spread. Chop is your jackpot — coins burst at every fill.

03 · SURVIVE

Manage inventory

A one-directional move fills only one side and piles up signed inventory that marks against you. Re-center to follow price, widen to slow fills, or flatten to dump it — before the bar hits the redline.

SKIN-FREE CORE. The player's bet is setting a two-sided quote (spread width + skew) the oscillating live price must repeatedly cross, settled by cumulative round-trip spread captured minus the mark-to-market bleed of signed inventory — paid out if net PnL clears the bar before inventory breaches the risk limit. A single price touch is a liability (it loads inventory), not a reward — the inverse of every touch-to-win game. You root for chop and fear the trend, in either direction.
