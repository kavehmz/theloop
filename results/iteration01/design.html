How a round flows

01 · DRAW

Author your route

The dim grid to the right is the next 8 seconds. Drag a freehand centre-line where you think price will travel. A glowing ribbon inflates around it at your chosen width.

02 · PRICE

Width is your greed dial

The multiplier updates every frame from 1 / width × reach × boldness. A hair-thin thread through a sharp move pays huge; a fat lazy thread pays a little. Shave one more pixel… or don't.

03 · THREAD

Hold your breath

Commit and a now-line sweeps across your tunnel as live ticks print. Stay inside the walls and the charge bar fills, the ribbon glows hotter, payout climbs.

04 · SNAP / LAND

Bank it or break it

Touch a wall and the thread SNAPS — gone. Reach the end inside and it LANDS for stake × multiplier. Or CUT early to bank the partial. Your call, every second.

Why it's a Deriv game

THREADLINE is a path-shaped option: it generalises Deriv's "stays-between / ends-between / touch" barrier contracts into a freehand, time-varying corridor the player draws themselves. It needs only the live synthetic-index tick feed and the volatility model Deriv already runs to price width → multiplier.

Why it's original

Every saturated casino game bets a scalar — a number, a side, a multiplier-before-crash. THREADLINE bets a freehand 2-D shape over future time, scored by containment of the realised price path inside a player-drawn corridor whose width is a live continuous risk dial. You predict the route, not the result.
