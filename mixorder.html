The Loop · Claude's editorial ranking · mixed set

All 39 Deriv games, ranked best → worst

The full mixed ranking: the original 30 designs plus the 9 newest, which were designed and built end-to-end by the Fable 5 model — those carry a violet Fable chip. Ranked on: instant visual wow in the first screenful, addictive "one more round" pull, originality of the core bet, and clarity of the live-market mechanic. Rows 1–33 are keepers; the 6 below the divider are duplicates and below-bar designs, ranked among themselves. Previews are the live prototypes (scaled to fit) — click "open ↗" for the full-bleed game.

1
THREADLINE Keeper open ↗

Draw the price's future as a tunnel and watch the live line thread it — the single most iconic, instantly-grokkable, unmistakably-Deriv hook of the whole set, on a stunning neon hero. Still my #1.

2
SLIPSTREAM Keeper open ↗

Fly the volatility canyon hugging the price rail for multiplier — the most visceral arcade rush and one of the richest heroes in the gallery.

3
CAUSTIC Keeper Fable open ↗

Aim light cannons at a liquid-mercury price mirror and get paid where the reflections land — the most striking hero of the Fable wave, and a settlement (pure reflection geometry off the realized path) nothing else in the set touches.

4
COREBORE Keeper open ↗

Drill through price-carved strata and winch out before the cave-in — dense and gorgeous, with a perfectly weighted winch-or-push greed loop.

5
SENTE Keeper Fable open ↗

A board duel where candles print your opponent's stones and you ambush them with nine of yours — market-as-adversary is a genuinely new idea, and watching the tape walk into a trap you set feels fantastic.

6
REACTOR Keeper open ↗

Harvest volatility as plasma, eject before the containment ring blows — a tense, beautiful control-room built around one knife-edge decision.

7
APOAPSIS Keeper open ↗

Orbit a live-price gravity-star and hold your band on a fuel budget — the most spectacular concept of the original wave; crash inward or fly out, pure skill.

8
CRUX Keeper Fable open ↗

Climb the price wall and slam irreversible pitons to lock your altitude floor before the storm hits — one perfect verb, zero bust anxiety, pure ratchet greed.

9
TOLLGATE Keeper open ↗

Bid to own price levels, paid when price touches — the most Deriv-native and quietly addictive bet (HOLD the toll or FLIP the claim).

10
MURMUR Keeper Fable open ↗

Herd sixty starlings riding the price jetstream home with eight pressure pulses — the most alive, organic hero in the whole gallery, and stake-sizing by visible weather is real skill.

11
SLINGSHOT VEGA Keeper open ↗

Wind a slingshot from momentum and fire through a distance-gate ladder — punchy, legible, immediately fun.

12
DETONATE Keeper open ↗

Seed charges across price-space and let the live path ignite a chain — a genuinely fresh connectivity bet with a fireworks payoff.

13
HOTHOUSE Keeper Fable open ↗

A greenhouse where up-trends, sell-offs, volatility and calm each feed a different species, and you graft growth between them mid-round — lush, readable, and quietly deep.

14
RIPTIDE Keeper open ↗

The live price paints territory in your colour — beautiful liquid ink and an easy 'hold the most ground' read.

15
SILKLINE Keeper open ↗

Bet the full finishing order of a live market race — the most broadly accessible idea here; photo-finish drama anyone gets in seconds.

16
GAVEL Keeper Fable open ↗

Sealed-bid auctions against three readable rival temperaments for lots the next 15 seconds of tape will price — the winner's-curse sweat is a brand-new feeling in this set.

17
RECOIL Keeper Fable open ↗

Tether the spread between two coupled runners and harvest every snap-back through your anchor — the only game in the set whose bet object is the relationship between two markets.

18
WAKELINE Keeper open ↗

Pre-trace where price goes next, paid on fidelity — a clever 'call the future' hook with a lovely anticipatory lead-zone.

19
SYNDICATE Keeper Fable open ↗

Flip the table: you collect the premiums and the chart hunts YOUR strikes — the house-side inversion lands, with a real reinsurance-cap decision under it.

20
DEADLOCK Keeper open ↗

Stake the displacement of a bull/bear tug-of-war knot — clean two-force tension with a satisfying PUSH/LOCK greed lever.

21
SUMMIT FLAGS Keeper open ↗

Call where AND when the peak forms, paid by 2-D proximity — tense, deterministic, and very Deriv-native.

22
WINDLEASE Keeper Fable open ↗

Lease the market's next minutes and harvest the violence inside your windows — variance-swap-as-a-game, clean and original, with demand-inflated quotes adding a second read.

23
STARFORGE Keeper open ↗

Thread the price-comet through decaying stars to draw a constellation — elegant and rarity-chasing, a touch more cerebral.

24
SLUICE Keeper open ↗

Route price-velocity-as-fluid through a pipe network you author — strong build-a-contraption appeal, balancing burst vs dry.

25
BALLAST Keeper open ↗

Counterweight a beam as price torque and volatility quakes tip it — tactile and tense, banking height before the topple.

26
STOWLINE Keeper open ↗

Pack market-forged cargo into a vault for density — the comforting Tetris-fit compulsion, with a spill bust.

27
PULSEPOINT Keeper open ↗

Tap exactly on price reversals to chain a Flow combo — a tight, juicy rhythm loop (timing games just feel a touch familiar).

28
RAMPART Keeper open ↗

Fortify price-level walls against a market siege — meaty tower-defense, a little heavier to onboard.

29
OVERTONE Keeper open ↗

Tune your oscillator to phase-lock the market wave — a beautiful oscilloscope and deep skill, but a more niche fascination.

30
FLUXYARD Keeper open ↗

Sort live regime-parcels into the right chutes — clever and fast, though the conveyor reads a little utilitarian.

31
ASSAY Keeper open ↗

Blend price-facets to match a drifting target alloy — gorgeous molten visuals over a more cerebral proportion puzzle.

32
SPREADKEEPER Keeper open ↗

Make a market: capture the spread, bleed on trends — the cleverest and most Deriv-true, but the most 'trading sim' and least casual.

33
EMBERWELL Keeper open ↗

Place a band and bank the time price dwells inside — a clean original primitive, but the quietest moment-to-moment agency.

▼ below the bar — not counted toward the 33 keepers
34
PRISMA Duplicate open ↗

Refract price-as-light into a claimable spectrum — one of the prettiest of all, but the bet de-skins to TOLLGATE+RIPTIDE, so it's a duplicate rather than a keeper.

35
DEEPLINE Duplicate open ↗

Cast, hook and fight the price-fish on a tension line — polished and atmospheric, but a recombination of TOLLGATE's touch + DEADLOCK's tension.

36
RICOCHET Below bar open ↗

Price-driven pinball with a peak-combo payout — fun and slick, but the combo settlement overlaps PULSEPOINT inside a saturated genre.

37
TICKER FORGE Below bar open ↗

Draft market-minted cards into a hand — sharp UI, but the scoring de-skins straight to poker hand-ranking.

38
EMBERWATCH Below bar open ↗

Triage many decaying embers across markets — a genuinely novel parallel-attention core, sunk by a thin, empty hero.

39
ECHO LADDER Below bar open ↗

Replay a price-authored note chain from memory — at heart a Simon reskin, on a sparse hero; the weakest of the set.

Editorial order — reasonable people will disagree on the middle. Three of the top five newcomers (CAUSTIC, SENTE, CRUX) are Fable builds; THREADLINE keeps the crown. See also: full gallery · the Fable set · original-30 ranking.
